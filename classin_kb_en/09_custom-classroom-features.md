## Not Auto-Exiting the Classroom

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
Teaching staff can enable or disable, in the Backend Management System, whether mobile devices automatically exit the classroom. Once enabled, if a user attending class on a phone or tablet views another app outside of ClassIn for more than 3 minutes, ClassIn will not automatically exit the classroom.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings at the top.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Toggle Settings.
6. Under "Mobile Auto-Exit Classroom Settings," turn "Don't auto-exit the classroom after leaving the ClassIn app for 3 minutes on mobile" on or off.
7. After finishing, click Next or Confirm.
8. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

## Default Cloud Drive Display Path

### What is the "teacher's default Cloud Drive display path"?
The "teacher's default Cloud Drive display path" refers to the page that first appears when a teacher opens the Cloud Drive tool in the classroom. The default path can be set to "My Cloud Drive" or "Authorized Resources." "My Cloud Drive" holds courseware or materials the teacher has uploaded themselves; "Authorized Resources" holds shared courseware the school has authorized the teacher to use, or resource courseware the teacher obtained from the ClassIn Resource Center.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
Based on teacher habits or school requirements, the school can change the default page teachers see when opening the Cloud Drive in the classroom. The default page can be set to "My Cloud Drive" or "Authorized Resources." "My Cloud Drive" holds courseware or materials the teacher has uploaded themselves; "Authorized Resources" holds shared courseware the school has authorized the teacher to use, or resource courseware the teacher obtained from the ClassIn Resource Center.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings at the top.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Toggle Settings.
6. Under "Teacher-Side Cloud Drive Display Settings," choose Authorized Resources or My Cloud Drive.
7. After finishing, click Next or Confirm.
8. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

## Changing the "Small Blackboard" Participant Cap

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
In the classroom, teachers can use the "Text Small Blackboard" and "Drawing Small Blackboard" for teaching, but each Small Blackboard can only be used by 35 students at once. If more than 35 students are online in real time, the teacher cannot use the Small Blackboard feature. If a teacher wants to use it in a classroom with more than 35 students, teaching staff can raise the Small Blackboard participant cap to 50 or 75 in the Backend Management System, allowing more students to use the tool simultaneously.

### Usage Notes
The Small Blackboard tool supports up to 35 students by default; teaching staff can raise this cap to 50 or 75 in the Backend Management System. Note that as the supported number of participants increases, the performance requirements for teachers' and students' devices increase as well — please adjust this setting carefully.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings at the top.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Parameter Settings.
6. Under "Small Blackboard Participant Settings," change the cap and click Confirm.
7. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

## Editing the Shortcuts Below the Camera

### What is the "toolbar below the camera"?
The "toolbar below the camera" refers to the action panel below teachers' and students' camera feeds in the classroom. It contains several clickable buttons, and teaching staff can add or edit its configuration in the Backend Management System. The toolbar must keep at least one button, and can show up to four. During class, the teacher or teaching staff can click the buttons below their own or a student's camera feed to control that student's camera and microphone, or to perform actions like awarding trophies or granting authorization.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
The toolbar below the teacher's camera in the classroom can hold up to four buttons. However, actual teaching may require up to nine different button functions. If a teacher needs to use other button types, teaching staff can swap and rearrange the toolbar buttons in the Backend Management System.

### Usage Notes
#### Shortcut buttons below the camera for "Teacher" / "Co-teacher":

- **Reset video avatars:** returns all camera feeds on the blackboard to the on-stage seats.
- **Mute all:** turns off the microphones of all students on stage.
- **Send all off stage:** moves all students currently on stage back off stage.
- **Authorize on-stage students:** grants classroom-tool permissions to all students currently on stage.
- **Reward on-stage students:** sends a trophy to all students currently on stage.
- **Swap on-stage students:** swaps the positions of all students currently on stage with those off stage.
- **Fill blackboard with student video:** displays all on-stage students' camera feeds at an even size on the large blackboard.
- **Revoke all authorization:** revokes classroom-tool permissions from every authorized student.
- **Auto-authorize students on stage:** the system automatically grants classroom-tool control permissions to all students currently on stage.

#### Shortcut buttons below the camera for "Student":

- **Authorize student:** the teacher grants an individual student permission to use teaching tools.
- **Mute student:** the teacher mutes an individual student's microphone.
- **Reward student:** the teacher sends a trophy to an individual student.
- **Move student off stage:** moves a specific on-stage student back off stage.
- **Private chat:** a private video chat between the teacher and a student.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings at the top.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Toggle Settings.
6. Under "Toolbar Below Avatar Settings," edit the shortcuts.
7. After finishing, click Next or Confirm.
8. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

In the classroom, the teacher and co-teacher cannot normally end class early — if class finishes ahead of schedule, students and the teacher can only leave the classroom on their own, which may leave some students still in the classroom. With this enabled, when the teacher finishes early, the teacher or co-teacher can choose "End Class for Everyone," which removes all students and teachers from the classroom and prevents them from re-entering — effectively ending class early.

## Enabling/Disabling the "Class Extension" Feature

### What is "Class Extension"?
"Class Extension" refers to a feature in ClassIn that lets the teacher or co-teacher extend a class session as it's about to end, ensuring the lesson content is fully covered.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
Teaching staff can enable the class extension feature in the Backend Management System. In every classroom using this configuration, as class is about to end, the teacher or co-teacher can choose to extend the class time to better complete the lesson.

### Usage Notes
Once teaching staff enable the class extension feature in the Backend Management System, a prompt will appear in the classroom 3–8 minutes before class is scheduled to end, asking the teacher or co-teacher whether to extend the class. They can choose "Extend 15 minutes," "Extend 30 minutes," or "Decide later." If the teacher chooses to extend, billing for the class session is adjusted accordingly. If they choose "Decide later," the extension prompt can be opened again within the 3–8 minute window before class ends.

The class can be extended multiple times, but the total class session length cannot exceed 24 hours.

The choice made by either teacher in the prompt is synced between them.

### Notes
After enabling a newly configured custom classroom setting, teachers must exit and re-enter the classroom for the updated configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Toggle Settings.
6. Under "Class Extension Settings," turn "Allow teachers to extend the class session" on or off (click Next or Confirm after making changes).
7. Enable the configured custom classroom setting.

## Enabling/Disabling "Auto-Authorization on the Blackboard"

### What is "Auto-Authorization on the Blackboard"?
The "Auto-Authorization on the Blackboard" feature in the Backend Management System automatically grants a student classroom-tool permissions when the teacher moves them from an on-stage seat to the large blackboard. When the teacher moves the student back from the blackboard to an on-stage seat, the authorization is automatically revoked.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
To save teachers from having to manually authorize students every time during teaching, we added student auto-authorization. When a teacher moves a student from an on-stage seat to the blackboard, the student's authorization is automatically turned on; when the student is moved back to an on-stage seat, authorization is automatically turned off — no extra steps required. If you need this feature, teaching staff can configure it in the Backend Management System.

### Usage Notes
If a teacher double-clicks a student's camera feed on an on-stage seat to move them to the large blackboard area, the student will NOT be auto-authorized — all other methods of moving support auto-authorization.

If a teacher double-clicks a student's camera feed on the blackboard to move them back to an on-stage seat, the student's authorization will NOT be auto-revoked — all other methods of moving support auto-revocation.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings at the top.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Toggle Settings.
6. Under "Chat Window Settings," turn "Auto-authorize when a student's camera feed is dragged onto the blackboard area" and "Auto-revoke authorization when a student's camera feed returns to an on-stage seat" on or off.
7. After finishing, click Next or Confirm.
8. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

## Classroom Scrolling Watermark

### What is the "scrolling watermark"?
The "scrolling watermark" is an anti-recording feature used in the ClassIn classroom. It scrolls a student's phone number across the screen at a set speed so that class content cannot be secretly captured by screen recording and traced back. When enabled, each student can only see their own phone number in their own classroom view, not anyone else's.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
Teaching staff can enable the student scrolling watermark feature in the Backend Management System. In every classroom using this configuration, every student and auditor will see a randomly moving phone number on their own screen during class, corresponding to that student's own phone number. This effectively deters students from recording class content and sharing it with others. If a student still records the video, the school can identify the person responsible from the phone number shown in the recording.

### Usage Notes
Each student can only see their own phone number on their own screen — they cannot see other students' phone numbers.

The scrolling watermark does not appear on the teacher's screen in the classroom.

To disable the scrolling watermark, contact teaching staff to turn it off in the Backend Management System.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings at the top.
4. Create or edit a custom classroom configuration.
5. Go to Toggle Settings.
6. Under "Student and Auditor Classroom Scrolling Watermark Settings," turn on or off "After a student enters the classroom, their nickname and phone number will move randomly around the classroom."
7. After finishing, click Next or Confirm.
8. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

## Classroom Anti-Recording Protection

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### When to use this
When teaching staff enable the classroom anti-recording feature in the Backend Management System, in every classroom using that configuration, students (but not the teacher or co-teacher) will be unable to use any screen-recording software to capture the classroom view. This helps prevent teaching content from being illegally captured or distributed, protecting intellectual property. Note: with this feature enabled, students attending on a phone or tablet also cannot mirror the classroom view to another device.

### Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, teachers need to exit and re-enter the classroom for the new configuration to take effect.

### Related Articles
"Adding a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Editing a Custom Classroom Configuration"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### What is "Advanced Mirror Mode"?
There are two places in ClassIn where mirror mode can be set: one in the classroom, and one in the Backend Management System — and they are fundamentally different. If a user only enables mirror mode within the classroom, only that user will see their own camera feed flipped left-right. ## Default Classroom

### What is the "default classroom"?
Classroom settings in the "Backend Management System" differ from classroom settings within the ClassIn app itself. Classroom settings in the Backend Management System are mainly used to enable, disable, and customize certain in-class features for classrooms within the app, while classroom settings within the app configure the basic audio/video functionality of each classroom. In the Backend Management System, teaching staff can choose between two types of classroom settings: System Classroom Settings and Custom Classroom Settings. System Classroom Settings cannot be changed, while Custom Classroom Settings can be personalized based on teaching needs — for example, setting the classroom background, features, and parameters — to provide a better teaching experience. So classroom settings in the Backend Management System and classroom settings within the app are two separate, non-conflicting features. If teaching staff do not assign a specific classroom setting to a "course (class)" when configuring it, every classroom under that course (class) will automatically use the default classroom setting.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "School Settings" permission

### Usage Notes
The "Default Classroom Setting" cannot be used in self-study rooms.

If teaching staff select a specific "Classroom Setting" for a "course (class)" when creating it, that course (class) will no longer use the "Default Classroom Setting" from the Backend Management System.
