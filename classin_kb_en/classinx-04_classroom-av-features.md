## ClassInX Classroom Audio/Video Features

# I. Quickly Adjusting Camera, Microphone, and Speaker

## Usage Notes
Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock and control their own microphone and camera. Clicking the microphone icon on the right side of the dock quickly turns the microphone on or off; if the on-stage seats area is hidden, clicking the camera icon on the right side of the dock lets you go on or off stage yourself; if the on-stage seats area is shown, clicking the camera icon on the right side of the dock turns your own camera on or off.

To switch to a different audio/video device or adjust audio volume, go to Settings on the far right of the dock. You can also do this under Additional Settings → Audio Settings.

# II. Adjusting the Camera

## 1. Switching Cameras

### Usage Notes
Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Video" to switch camera devices.

## 2. Adding a Network Camera

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Video" to add a network camera. In the Add Network Camera window, just fill in the network camera's name and URL. Editing or deleting already-added network cameras is also supported.

## 3. Adjusting Network Camera Volume

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Video" to adjust the network camera's volume. Users themselves cannot hear that network camera's sound — this setting adjusts the volume other users in the classroom hear.

## 4. Rotating the Camera

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Video" to rotate the camera. Clicking the rotate button once rotates the camera image 90 degrees to the right. Network cameras do not support rotation or mirroring.

## 5. Camera Mirroring

By default, users see their own camera feed reversed relative to reality. If you'd like to adjust this, you can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Video" to turn on camera mirroring.

Also, by default, other users in the classroom also see your camera feed mirrored. If a user has enabled "Camera Mirroring" and wants other classroom users to also see this mirrored feed (that is, so what they see of themselves matches what others see of them), they can contact the academic staff with access to the "Backend Management System" and ask them to add a classroom skin under 【School Settings → Classroom Settings】 in the backend, enabling the 【Camera Mirroring Mode Setting】 feature within that skin, then apply that skin to the class. Once this is set, every user in the classroom needs to exit and re-enter class for it to take effect.

Network cameras do not support rotation or mirroring.

## 6. Camera Virtual Background

Windows 10 and higher supports setting a virtual camera background within ClassInX software. You can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Virtual Background" to set it.

Users can choose from the system's built-in virtual backgrounds, or upload a local image as a virtual background. Uploaded images must be in PNG format, with up to 10 uploaded at once. There's no size limit on individual images, but if a single image exceeds 2MB, the system automatically compresses its resolution down to 60% of the original.

# III. Adjusting the Microphone

## 1. Switching Microphones

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to switch microphone devices.

## 2. Testing the Microphone

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to test whether the microphone is working normally.

Click the "Test" button next to the microphone device — a 10-second countdown will begin. During the countdown, speak (for example, count from 1 to 10); if the microphone indicator shows a green wave moving while you speak, and you can hear your own voice, the microphone is working normally. If the indicator shows a yellow wave, the microphone volume is too low — try speaking closer to the microphone, or raise the microphone volume. If there's no wave movement while speaking at all, try switching to a different microphone device, or fix the microphone and then restart your computer/large display.

## 3. Adjusting Microphone Volume

If the microphone's wave indicator shows yellow while you speak, or shows no movement at all, you can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to raise the microphone volume, or enable "Auto-Adjust Microphone Volume."

If manually raising the microphone volume, we don't recommend setting it too high, since this can introduce noise and other issues.

While "Auto-Adjust Microphone Volume" is enabled, manually adjusting microphone volume is not supported.

Also, once "Auto-Adjust Microphone Volume" is enabled, "Background Noise Reduction" is enabled by default along with it, and "Music Mode" is disabled by default. Conversely, once "Auto-Adjust Microphone Volume" is disabled, "Background Noise Reduction" stays enabled and "Music Mode" stays disabled — adjust these yourself if needed.

## 4. Background Noise Reduction

If the environment around a user is too noisy, they can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to enable "Background Noise Reduction." Once enabled, ambient noise will be automatically reduced, making it easier for other users in the classroom to hear speech clearly. We recommend keeping this enabled in most usage scenarios.

In a music scenario, you need to disable "Background Noise Reduction" before you can enable "Music Mode." Also, once "Auto-Adjust Microphone Volume" is enabled, "Background Noise Reduction" is enabled by default along with it.

### Notes
In a music scenario, once the teacher enables the classroom's "Music Mode," the "AI Noise Reduction" or "Background Noise Reduction" feature for every user in the classroom stops working — this is by design, to ensure instrument sound comes through smoothly.

## 5. Music Mode

When a class involves a professional music scenario (such as playing an instrument), we recommend the teacher tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to enable the classroom's "Music Mode."

"Music Mode" can only be enabled once both "Background Noise Reduction" and "Auto-Adjust Microphone Volume" are disabled. Once "Music Mode" is enabled, the system's noise reduction for voices and ambient noise will be weakened.

## 6. Auto-Enable Microphone on Entering the Classroom

If a user wants their microphone to turn on automatically every time they enter a ClassInX classroom on computer or large display, they can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to enable "Auto-Enable Microphone on Entering Classroom."

# IV. Adjusting the Speaker

## 1. Switching Speakers

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to switch speaker devices.

## 2. Testing the Speaker

Users can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to test whether the speaker is working normally.

Click the "Test" button next to the speaker device — a sound will play. If you can hear a drum sound, the speaker is working normally. If you don't hear anything, try raising the speaker volume, switching to a different speaker device, or fixing the speaker and then restarting your computer/large display.

## 3. Adjusting Speaker Volume

If the sound a user hears is too quiet or too loud, they can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Audio" to adjust the speaker volume.

# V. Hardware Fix (Camera/Microphone/Speaker)

**Fixing the camera:** if the local camera app on Windows shows the camera feed normally, but the image still won't display once you've selected that camera in ClassIn X, you can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Hardware Fix" in the bottom-left corner to fix the camera; we recommend restarting your computer/large display once the fix is complete.

**Fixing the microphone:** if the microphone works normally locally on Windows, but there's no green wave movement when speaking once you've selected that microphone in ClassIn X, you can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Hardware Fix" in the bottom-left corner to fix the microphone; we recommend restarting your computer/large display once the fix is complete.

**Fixing the speaker:** if sound plays normally locally on Windows, but clicking the "Test" button next to the speaker device in ClassIn X still produces no sound, you can tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock, then go to "Settings" → "Additional Settings" → "Hardware Fix" in the bottom-left corner to fix the speaker; we recommend restarting your computer/large display once the fix is complete.
