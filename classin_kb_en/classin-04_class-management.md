## Class AI Apps

## I. Class AI Agent Chat

### Applies to
Head teachers and class teachers.

Once the head teacher enables "Students can use AI apps" in the class's AI App Settings, students can use it too.

### When to use this
ClassIn 6.0 deeply integrates advanced AI technology to build a powerful "AI Agent Platform," designed to drive teaching innovation and comprehensively improve teaching efficiency through intelligent tools. Teachers can flexibly enable and manage various official AI apps based on their specific teaching needs — like having a team of professional smart teaching assistants.

### Usage Notes
Class teachers, and class students who are allowed to use AI apps, can chat with AI agents, including AI Teaching Assistant, AI Learning Analytics, and any agents added independently from the AgentIn agent store.

**AI Teaching Assistant:** answers student questions based on the class's teaching resources (such as courseware and documents), recommends relevant content, or provides hints toward an answer — offering students round-the-clock personalized Q&A and resource guidance, supporting self-directed learning, and reducing the burden of repetitive Q&A on teachers.

**AI Learning Analytics:** dynamically analyzes classroom interaction, assignment completion, and other learning data to generate visual reports on knowledge mastery, learning progress, and classroom participation — helping teachers precisely understand student status and make informed decisions for personalized, differentiated teaching.

### Steps

**Computer/tablet**
1. Go to Home.
2. Find and enter the class.
3. Click AI Agent.
4. Start chatting.

## II. Creating an AI Agent for the Class

Head teachers and class teachers can use a computer to go to the class's AI Apps, then open the AgentIn agent store to create an agent.

You can create an AI app by selecting an existing template. Once created, you can publish the agent to the agent store for everyone to use, or set its visibility to "only me." Published agents can be found and used under "Created by Me." Agents published to the store can be found on the homepage by filtering by the store category you set before publishing. Published agents can be edited, deleted, or unpublished from "Created by Me."

You can also import agents already published on platforms such as Flowin, Coze, Yuanqi, and FastGPT. Imported agents can be edited or deleted from "Created by Me."

In addition, any agents you create or import will also appear by default in "My Favorites."

Tablets and phones do not support creating AI agents.

### 1. Creating an AI Agent

#### Steps

**Computer**
1. Find AI Apps and click "+."
2. Click "Created by Me," then click "Create AI App" in the top-right corner.
3. Configure the AI app info and template, then click Next.
4. Once configuration and testing are complete, click Publish.
5. Set the visibility and other details, then click Confirm Publish.
6. Go to "Created by Me" to view the agent you created.

## III. Adding an AI Agent to the Class

Head teachers and class teachers can use a computer or tablet to go to the class's AI Apps, open the AgentIn agent store, and add an existing agent to the class for teachers and students to use.

The first time you open the AgentIn agent store, you'll need to select your role, grade level, and subject; the system will recommend suitable agents based on your selections. These selections can be changed anytime from the bottom-left corner of the AgentIn agent store.

On the Home page, you can browse agents listed in the agent store; you can filter by role and subject/grade level in the top-left corner. Click into an agent to view its trial page, where you can add it to your class.

Agents you've created or imported appear by default in "My Favorites" — click into one there to add it to your class.

Adding AI agents is not supported on phones.

Find and click the agent, then click Add.

## Class Announcements

## I. What Are Announcements?

Teachers can post announcements in a class for important notices.

## II. Creating an Announcement

### Applies to
Head teachers and class teachers.

### Usage Notes
Teachers can post announcements within a class, and can sync an announcement across multiple classes at once (as long as the teacher posting it holds a teacher role in each of those classes). To make sure every student sees the announcement, you can enable the "require students to confirm they've read it" feature when creating the announcement. Announcements can also be pinned — a pinned announcement appears at the top of the class chat group so teachers and students can find it quickly.

Announcement content can include text and files. File limits: up to 15 image attachments, up to 15 video attachments, and the combined total of local courseware, cloud drive courseware, and links cannot exceed 15. Each video or other file must be under 500MB, and each image under 25MB.

### What is co-creation?
The co-creation feature is similar to a class bulletin board. Once a class uses co-creation, class members can jointly view and edit its content.

### Applies to
Head teachers and class teachers.

Class students can view co-creation content, and if the head teacher enables "Allow students to edit co-creation documents" in the co-creation settings, students can also add to and edit co-creation pages.

### Usage Notes
Students and teachers with edit permission can create and edit co-creation pages. Besides basic features like typing text and using tables, co-creation pages support uploading images, audio, video, and files, and using AI features for writing, drawing, and more.

## Creating / Dissolving / Leaving a Class

## I. Creating a Class

### What is a class?
A class is the basic educational unit a school organizes to achieve a specific educational goal. It includes students and teachers, and promotes student learning and growth through teaching and group activities. On the ClassIn online education platform, classes serve several key roles:

**The basic unit of teaching activity:** Classes are the basic unit for conducting teaching activities — teachers can create courses within a class, teach online, and manage learning. Students can access teaching resources and join discussions through the class.

**Encouraging student interaction and collaboration:** In the class chat area, students can communicate and collaborate with other members or teachers through text messages, discussion, and other interactions, improving learning outcomes.

**Making it easier to manage learning progress and content:** Teaching staff can create different series of courses within a class, making it easier to organize and manage students' learning progress and content across different courses. Students can find the courses they need within the class and study according to their own plans.

### Applies to
In ClassIn, any user can create a class, though the settings and features available may differ depending on the ClassIn edition. Classes are mainly created by school teaching staff, head teachers, or course teachers, who have more permissions and features available in ClassIn.

### When to use this
Creating a class helps organize and manage student learning activities more efficiently. A class brings students of the same or similar grade level together to study and communicate in a unified environment. By creating a class, teachers can better understand and track student progress and provide timely guidance and support. At the same time, a class gives students a shared platform for learning, encouraging collaboration and mutual support.

In addition, a class helps foster students' sense of community and teamwork, giving them a chance to build well-rounded skills and social abilities. Creating classes is therefore important for establishing an orderly, positive learning atmosphere and improving the quality of education and teaching.

### Usage Notes
A class can offer a variety of teaching features, including but not limited to:

**Chat:** In "Chat," all class members can send text messages, voice messages (phone and tablet only), stickers, images, and files from their computer or cloud drive, encouraging interaction.

**Courses & Activities:** Teachers can create units and activities under a course. Activities include class sessions, recorded courses, assignments, quizzes (exam papers), answer sheets, discussions, learning materials, and AI Speaking Cards — providing diverse content and formats for teaching.

**Grades:** In "Grades," teachers can view students' grades and competency scores across various learning activities, giving a full picture of student progress and ability, to support individual tutoring and further guidance.

## Class Member Management

## I. Sharing the Class Sign-Up Entry / Adding Students to a Class

### Applies to
Head teachers and teachers can share the class sign-up entry with users who want to join.

### Prerequisites
Before sharing the class sign-up entry, the head teacher must enable "Open Class Enrollment" in Class Management.

### When to use this
Sharing the "class sign-up entry" means providing teachers and students with a self-service way to join a class. Through this entry, teachers and students who aren't yet in the class can join it on their own. This entry can be a "class QR code" or a "class invitation code." The goal is to make sign-up more flexible and convenient — for schools or organizations, sharing the class sign-up entry can effectively reduce manual work and improve management efficiency.

### Usage Notes
Any teacher member of the class can share the "class QR code" or "class invitation code" with teachers and students who need to join.

Teachers and students who haven't joined the class yet can use the scan feature in the ClassIn app to scan the "class QR code" shared by a class teacher, or tap the link in the "class invitation code" shared by a class teacher. This lets them sign up and join the class — new members join as students by default.

## II. Adding a Teacher to a Class

Applies to head teachers.

During class management and instruction, you may need multiple teachers to help the head teacher manage the class, or to take on different teaching roles during lessons. To ensure lessons run smoothly and classroom order is maintained, the head teacher should add relevant teachers to the class in advance.
