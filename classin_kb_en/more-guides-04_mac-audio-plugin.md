## Mac Audio Plugin (ClassIn Audio Device)

### Usage Notes
ClassIn Audio Device is an audio plugin used in two scenarios when a teacher uses ClassIn on a Mac.

**Scenario 1:** while using the "Screen Share" tool, to share sound from the local computer, the teacher needs to install the ClassIn Audio Device plugin — only then can local computer sound be shared with everyone else in the classroom.

**Scenario 2:** while recording class, if the teacher wants the sound from their computer desktop captured in the class playback video, they need to manually select "System Sound" under "Recording Mode" in the app settings. Once this mode is enabled, the teacher needs to install the ClassIn Audio Device plugin — only then can local computer sound be captured during recording.

### Notes
macOS version required to install the audio plugin: macOS 10.13 or higher.

The ClassIn Audio Device plugin only needs to be installed once.

Windows computers do not need to install the ClassIn Audio Device plugin.

Once ClassIn Audio Device is installed, your computer's speaker defaults to ClassIn Audio Device. If the teacher wants to use a different speaker, please close ClassIn first, then switch the system's speaker device.

### Installing ClassIn Audio Device During Screen Sharing

**Step 1:** when a teacher uses the screen share tool, a window will appear asking for their username and password. In this window, the teacher just needs to enter their computer's username and password to automatically complete installation of the ClassIn Audio Device plugin. (Please don't cancel the installation, or sound won't be shareable during screen sharing.)

**Step 2:** to check whether ClassIn Audio Device was installed successfully, the teacher can click the audio icon in the top-right corner of their computer and check whether there's a "ClassIn Audio Device" option. If it's there, the installation succeeded. At this point, the user can select the ClassIn Audio Device as the sound output in their computer's sound settings.

### Installing ClassIn Audio Device Before Recording Local Sound

**Step 1:** the teacher goes to "Recording Mode" in the app settings and checks "System Sound."

**Step 2:** once they enter the classroom and start recording, the teacher will be prompted to install the audio plugin — click Confirm on the prompt.

**Step 3:** ClassIn will show the teacher a dialog asking for their username and password; the teacher enters their computer's username and password to automatically complete installation of the ClassIn Audio Device plugin. (Please don't cancel the installation, or local computer sound won't be captured into the playback video while recording.)

**Step 4:** to check whether ClassIn Audio Device was installed successfully, the teacher can click the audio icon in the top-right corner of their computer and check whether there's a "ClassIn Audio Device" option. If it's there, the installation succeeded.
