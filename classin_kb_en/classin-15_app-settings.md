## Switching Accounts

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
Once logged into ClassIn, if you want to switch to a different account, you can do this: go to Settings in the bottom-left corner of the software, select "Switch Account," and this logs you out of the currently logged-in account. On phone or tablet, you can only log out first, then log back in with a different account.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Click Switch Account.
3. Click Confirm.

## Exiting the App

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
Once you're done with an activity or class, you can proactively exit ClassIn to shut down the software's related background processes. On computer, click your avatar in the bottom-left corner, then click Exit App; on phone or tablet, click your avatar on the home page, then click Log Out.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Click Exit App.
3. Click Confirm.

#### Phone
1. Go to Home in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Tap Log Out.

#### Tablet
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.

## System Permission Management

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
ClassIn's privacy settings include a system permission management feature. Users can use this feature to view and manage, all in one place, the various device permissions the app has been granted (such as camera, microphone, and photo album access). Once you tap into this feature, the page jumps directly to your phone or tablet's system settings screen, where you can quickly turn each permission on or off.

### Notes
Users on phone or tablet can view and access system permission management within ClassIn; on computer, you'll need to go to your computer's system settings to check which permissions ClassIn has been granted.

### Steps

#### Phone
1. Go to Home in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Tap App Settings.
4. Go to Privacy Settings.
5. Go to System Permission Management.
6. View or go to the permission management page.
7. Turn a permission on or off.

#### Tablet
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.

## Upgrading ClassIn

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
The new version of ClassIn not only improves the user experience but also adds brand-new features. To make your teaching or learning go more smoothly, we recommend upgrading ClassIn to the latest version.

### Notes
If you run into an error while upgrading ClassIn that prevents the upgrade from completing normally, you can uninstall your currently installed copy of ClassIn, then re-download and install the ClassIn client from the official website, www.eeo.cn.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to About.
3. Upgrade, or Check for Updates.

#### Phone
1. Go to Home in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Go to About.
4. If a new version is available, tap Upgrade to update the app to the latest version.

#### Tablet
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.

## Tablet/Phone Mode

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
A tablet meeting the screen-size requirements will show the IM interface (outside the classroom) in tablet mode by default. If you run into laggy, unresponsive controls or slow page loading while using the software in tablet mode, we recommend switching to phone mode, either in App Settings after logging in or on the login page. Switching from phone mode back to tablet mode is also supported.

## I. Switching to Phone Mode from App Settings

### Steps

#### Tablet

3. Enable Phone Mode.
4. Click Restart Now.

## II. Switching to Tablet Mode from App Settings

1. Go to Home.
2. Click your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Turn off Phone Mode.

## III. Switching to Phone Mode at Login

1. Click "Use Phone Mode" below the login page.

## IV. Switching to Tablet Mode at Login

1. Click "Use Tablet Mode" below the login page.

## Recording Codec

### Applies to
The teacher recording the class.

### Usage Notes
On Windows computers, the recording codec comes in two types: hardware encoding and legacy mode. By default, recording uses hardware encoding; we don't recommend individually changing this setting without good reason — if you have a specific need, we suggest consulting ClassIn support first.

Also, for the same recording scenario, hardware encoding produces a larger recorded file than legacy mode.

Changing the recording codec only takes effect the next time you enter the classroom and start recording.

### Notes
Phone and tablet do not have a "Recording Codec" setting.

Mac computers do not have a "Recording Codec" feature.

### Steps

#### Computer (Windows)
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change the Recording Codec.

## Recording Mode (Software/System Sound)

### Applies to
The teacher recording the class.

### Usage Notes
Teachers recording on computer can choose which sound range to capture based on their teaching-recording needs — either system sound or software sound, with software sound being the default. If the resulting class playback video's sound is too quiet or you can't hear it at all, try recording with software sound next time.

**Software sound:** only records sound within the classroom; sound from other software on your computer desktop won't be recorded. The teacher also needs to make sure the speaker selected in the classroom is working properly.

**System sound:** records all sound both within the classroom and from other software on your computer desktop — for example, QQ message alert sounds, WeChat message alert sounds, or video sound playing on a webpage. The teacher also needs to make sure the speaker selected in the classroom is working properly.

Also, changing the recording mode requires restarting the ClassIn app to take effect.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change the Recording Mode.

## Advanced Recording Mode (iOS)

### Applies to
The teacher recording the class.

### When to use this
When a teacher recording class is using an Apple iPhone or iPad, and needs to use screen sharing while recording the shared content and its sound, we recommend enabling Advanced Recording Mode before entering the classroom to record. In addition, if the classroom shows a "recording error" that interrupts recording and prevents it from continuing, the teacher can also try enabling Advanced Recording Mode and re-entering the classroom to record.

### Notes
Computer does not have an "Advanced Recording Mode" feature.

Phone and tablet (Android, HarmonyOS, or Google's system) do not have an "Advanced Recording Mode" feature.

### Steps

#### iPhone
1. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
2. Go to App Settings.
3. Go to Advanced Settings.
4. Turn Advanced Recording Mode on/off.

#### iPad
1. Tap your avatar in the bottom-left corner.

## Recorded Video Resolution (HD/Full HD)

### Applies to
The teacher recording the class.

### Usage Notes
Teachers recording on computer can adjust the recorded video's resolution, with two options:

**Full HD mode (1920 x 1080):** suited to scenarios that demand higher playback video quality; we recommend a computer with stronger performance, with a CPU of 6th-generation Intel i5 or higher. Also, if the recording area is set to the minimum-size classroom window, this mode cannot be selected.

**HD mode (1280 x 720):** the default mode, recommended for computers with weaker performance, with a CPU of Intel i5 or higher.

If the teacher's monitor resolution is lower than the selected recording resolution, the recording will use the lower resolution instead.

Changing the recorded video resolution only takes effect the next time you enter the classroom and start recording.

### Notes
For teachers recording on phone or tablet, the recorded video resolution is fixed and cannot be adjusted.

Compared to HD mode, Full HD mode produces a larger recorded video file, taking up more storage space, whether cloud or local.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change the Recorded Video Resolution.

## Recording Area (Minimum Size / Current Size / Entire Computer Desktop)

### Applies to
The teacher recording the class.

### Usage Notes
Teachers recording on computer can flexibly choose the recording area based on their computer's specs and recording needs. The recording area options are:

**Minimum-Size Classroom Window:** once enabled, the classroom window automatically shrinks to 1280 x 720px, and records what's inside the teacher's current classroom window; classroom window resizing is not supported — suited to lower-performance computers. During screen sharing, the shared area is recorded.

**Current-Size Classroom Window:** once enabled, records what's inside the teacher's current classroom window, and allows freely resizing the classroom window; a larger window demands more from your computer's CPU. During screen sharing, the shared area is recorded.

**Entire Computer Desktop:** once enabled, records everything the teacher does on the computer desktop where their classroom window is located; a larger monitor screen demands more from your computer's CPU.

If your device has weaker performance, we recommend "Minimum-Size Classroom Window" mode; if you need to teach full-screen or resize the window, choose "Current-Size Classroom Window" mode; if you need to record both the classroom view and desktop activity at the same time (for example, demonstrating by switching between multiple apps), you can enable "Entire Desktop Recording" mode.

Also, changing the recording area only takes effect the next time you enter the classroom and start recording.

### Notes
For teachers recording on phone or tablet, the recording area is fixed and cannot be adjusted.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change the Recording Area.

## Speaker

## I. Speaker Mode

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
If you run into the following issues while using ClassIn, try enabling "Legacy Mode 1" or "Legacy Mode 2" under "Speaker Mode" in ClassIn's settings. For phone/tablet users, try enabling "Speaker Mode" in settings:

If a Windows computer user hasn't muted their speaker in the classroom but still can't hear any sound in the classroom, and the issue persists after using "Fix Hardware Driver," try enabling "Legacy Mode 1" or "Legacy Mode 2" under "Speaker Mode" in the app settings.

If an Android phone or tablet user hasn't disabled their speaker in the classroom, and the speaker volume is normal, but they still can't hear any sound in the classroom, try enabling "Speaker Mode" in settings.

### Notes
After a Windows computer user changes "Speaker Mode," ClassIn must be restarted for the change to take effect.

### Steps

#### Computer (Windows)
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change the Speaker Mode.

#### Phone/Tablet (Android)
1. Go to Home in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Go to App Settings.
4. Go to Advanced Settings.
5. Turn Speaker Mode on or off.

## II. Fixing the Speaker

If a Windows computer user's speaker test plays sound normally locally via the small speaker icon in the bottom-right corner, but there's no sound from the speaker in the ClassIn classroom, they can use the "Fix Hardware Driver" feature to fix the speaker; we recommend restarting the computer once the fix is complete.

Phone and tablet don't have "hardware driver fix" — if you run into this kind of issue on phone or tablet, try restarting the device instead.

Mac computers don't have a "Fix Hardware Driver" feature — if this happens on a Mac, try restarting the computer instead.

1. Go to System Settings.
2. Scroll the right-side scrollbar all the way down.
3. Under Hardware Driver Fix, click Fix.
4. (If a firewall prompt blocks it, click Allow or Yes.) Click Fix Speaker; once it reports the fix is complete, we recommend restarting your computer.

## Camera

## I. Camera Mode

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
If your computer's local camera app shows your camera feed fine, but you run into the following issues while using ClassIn:

Clicking "Start Class" to enter the classroom causes ClassIn to freeze and become unresponsive, unable to enter the classroom normally, with the mouse cursor spinning continuously.

After turning on the camera in the classroom, the camera toggle keeps jumping back to "disabled."

After turning on the camera in the classroom, you see the message: "Camera is being used by another app." You've confirmed no other app is using the camera, confirmed you've exited any security/antivirus software on your computer, and used "Fix Hardware Driver" to fix the camera — but your own video still won't display properly.

For any of the issues above, we recommend trying to check "Legacy Mode" under "Camera Mode" in ClassIn's app settings. After changing "Camera Mode," ClassIn must be restarted for the setting to take effect.

### Notes
Phone and tablet do not have a "Camera Mode" setting.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change the Camera Mode.

## II. Fixing the Camera

If a Windows computer user's local camera app shows the camera feed fine, but they run into the following camera issues in the ClassIn classroom, this feature can also help fix the camera:

The camera video appears green, gray, or black.

The camera video is glitchy/garbled.

The camera reports being occupied by another app.

The camera automatically switches to disabled once turned on.

We recommend restarting your computer once the camera fix is complete.

Phone and tablet don't have "hardware driver fix" — if you run into this kind of issue on phone or tablet, try restarting the device instead.

Mac computers don't have a "Fix Hardware Driver" feature. If this happens on a Mac, first check whether "Camera" access is authorized for ClassIn in your Mac's local settings, and grant authorization if not. If it's already authorized and the issue persists, try restarting your computer.

#### Computer (Windows)
1. Go to System Settings.
2. Scroll the right-side scrollbar all the way down.
3. Under Hardware Driver Fix, click Fix.
4. (If a firewall prompt blocks it, click Allow or Yes.) Click Fix Camera; once it reports the fix is complete, we recommend restarting your computer.

## III. Video Software Decoder

When a user takes class on an Android phone or tablet (Android, HarmonyOS, or Google's system), if their own camera feed looks fine to themselves, but other classroom members can't see them properly — for example, seeing a glitchy/garbled image — the user can try enabling "Video Software Decoder" and disabling "Video Hardware Decoder" in app settings to fix the issue.

Computer does not have a "Video Software Decoder" feature.

iPhone/iPad devices do not have a "Video Software Decoder" feature.

#### Phone/Tablet (Android)
1. Go to Home in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Go to App Settings.
4. Go to Advanced Settings.
5. Turn Video Software Decoder on or off.

## IV. Video Hardware Decoder

Computer does not have a "Video Hardware Decoder" feature.

iPhone/iPad devices do not have a "Video Hardware Decoder" feature.

1. Turn Video Hardware Decoder on or off.

## ClassIn App Notifications

## I. Class Notifications

### Applies to
The class session's lead teacher, co-teacher(s), and students.

### When to use this
If you want to receive class reminder notifications in the ClassIn app, you can enable the "Class Notifications" feature in ClassIn's settings.

### Usage Notes
Once "Class Notifications" is enabled in the ClassIn app, it will push a class reminder to the teacher 80 minutes and 20 minutes before class officially starts; students receive a class reminder 70 minutes and 10 minutes before class officially starts.

In addition, on computer, ClassIn needs to be logged in; on phone or tablet, ClassIn needs to be logged in and running in the background, and the device's own system settings need to allow ClassIn to send "notifications." On computer, the notification appears as a pop-up in the bottom-right corner; on phone or tablet, the pop-up style is determined by the device's own notification settings.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to System Settings.
3. Turn Class Notifications on or off.

#### Phone
1. Go to Home in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
3. Go to App Settings.
4. Go to New Message Notifications.

#### Tablet
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.

## II. Learning Activity Notifications

Teachers and students with an activity.

If you want to receive notification reminders in the ClassIn app for LMS learning activities (class sessions, recorded courses, assignments, quizzes, check-ins, discussions, answer sheets, learning materials, and so on), you can enable the "Learning Activity Notifications" feature in ClassIn's settings.

Once "Learning Activity Notifications" is enabled in the ClassIn app, students will receive a push notification 1/3/7 days before an activity's due date, once a teacher grades or sends back the activity for revision, or when a teacher reminds students to submit the activity; teachers will receive a push notification once a student submits or revises an activity.

1. Turn Learning Activity Notifications on or off.

## III. New Message Notifications

Everyone.

Users on phone or tablet can turn New Message Notifications on or off within the ClassIn app.

In addition, you'll need to make sure ClassIn is logged in and running in the background, and that the device's own system settings allow ClassIn to send "notifications." The pop-up style shown on phone or tablet is determined by the device's own notification settings.

1. Turn New Message Notifications on or off.

## IV. New Message Alert Sound

Users on computer can turn the new-message alert sound on or off within the ClassIn app. If you're on phone or tablet, ClassIn does not let you configure the message alert sound — you'll need to configure it in the device's own system settings.

1. Turn the New Message Alert Sound on or off.
