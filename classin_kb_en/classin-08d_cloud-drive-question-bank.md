## Question Bank Center

## I. What Is the Question Bank Center?

The Question Bank Center gives users a wealth of question resources, including a personal question bank, an organization question bank, and ClassIn's official question bank. Users can choose different question types to add as needed, such as single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer, and comprehensive questions. A comprehensive question can contain several sub-questions, with sub-question types including single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and short-answer.

Users can add questions one at a time, or in bulk, whichever is more convenient. Once questions are added, users can also create an exam paper and add already-added questions to it, or add questions directly while creating the paper.

For teachers under institution/school teacher management, whether the organization question bank shows up under Question Bank Center in the ClassIn app's Space, and whether they can view and use its content, depends on their member-management permission for the relevant school level and subject.

Whether for personal study or organizational training, the Question Bank Center can meet a wide range of user needs, offering efficient, convenient question management and exam paper creation.

## II. My Question Bank

## 1. Adding a School Level and Subject

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
Users on computer or tablet can set up school levels and subjects in My Question Bank, making it easy to later categorize added questions under a specific school level and subject.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to Space.
2. Go to Question Bank Center.
3. Go to My Question Bank.
4. Add a school level / subject.

## 2. Adding Questions

When adding questions in My Question Bank, users can set up knowledge points or chapters as needed, then add questions under the corresponding knowledge point or chapter. When adding questions on computer or tablet, users can add several question types: single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer, and comprehensive questions (a comprehensive question can contain several sub-questions, supporting single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and short-answer sub-types). When adding questions on phone, only single-choice, multiple-choice, and true/false questions are supported. Once questions are added, users can delete, duplicate, forward, or move them to another subject. Teachers with organization question bank permission can also add questions to the organization question bank(s) they have access to.

When you need to add multiple questions at once, you can use the bulk-add feature on computer. First, download the Excel or Word template from the bulk-add window, then add your questions following the template's format. Once done, upload the completed template back into the bulk-add window. If you're already familiar with the bulk-add format, you can also directly copy and paste correctly formatted questions into the raw-question entry area within the bulk-add window.

#### Computer
1. Select a school level/subject and Questions.
2. Click "+ Add Question" / "Bulk Add Questions."

#### Tablet
1. Click "+ Add Question."

## 3. Adding an Exam Paper

Users can add an exam paper in My Question Bank. When creating a paper, you can select questions from My Question Bank to add to it, or add new questions directly while creating the paper; on computer, bulk-adding questions is also supported. Already-added papers can be duplicated, forwarded, or moved to a different school level or subject; on computer, you can also download a paper locally. Teachers with organization question bank permission can also add a paper to the corresponding organization question bank.

1. Select a school level/subject.
2. Select Exam Papers.
3. Click Add Exam Paper.

Click "+ Add Exam Paper."

Click "+" to add an exam paper.

## 4. Editing/Deleting Questions and Exam Papers

An already-added question can be edited, deleted, duplicated, forwarded to a ClassIn class or friend, moved to a different subject, or added to an organization question bank.

An already-added exam paper can be edited, duplicated, downloaded, deleted, forwarded to a ClassIn class or friend, moved to a different school level, used to create a quiz and publish it to another class, or have its questions added to an organization question bank.

### Notes
Phone only supports editing single-choice, multiple-choice, and true/false questions.

Neither phone nor tablet supports downloading an exam paper.

1. Select a school level/subject, Questions, or Exam Papers.
2. Find the question or paper, click Edit, or click "···" to delete it.

## 5. Adding a Question/Exam Paper to the Organization Question Bank

The school/institution's primary account, school/institution administrators with "Question Bank Center" permission, and teachers with "Operate" or "Manage" permission for the relevant school level/subject in the organization question bank.

Teachers with the relevant permission can add a question or exam paper from My Question Bank to the organization question bank.

1. Find the question or exam paper, then click Add to Organization Question Bank.

## 6. Compiling & Downloading an Exam Paper, and Creating a New Quiz

Everyone, or class teachers.

Questions and exam papers in "My Question Bank" support being added to the Question Basket, where you can view, delete, and reorder questions, and also compile & download a paper or create a new quiz.

**Compile & Download:** jumps to the exam paper page, supporting saving the paper; on computer, you can also download the paper locally.

**Create New Quiz:** jumps to the new-quiz page, where teachers can publish it to a class and save the paper to My Question Bank; on computer, you can also download the paper locally.

In addition, teachers can also download an exam paper's content directly and create a quiz under "My Question Bank → Exam Papers."

Phone does not support compiling & downloading a paper or creating a quiz.

Tablet supports creating a quiz and saving a paper to the "Exam Papers" section under "My Question Bank," but does not support downloading the paper's content.

1. Find the question or exam paper, then click Add to Question Basket, or click "···" next to a paper.
2. Compile & Download, or Create New Quiz.

## III. Organization Question Bank

The school/institution's primary account, and school/institution administrators with "Question Bank Center" permission.

Teachers with the relevant permission can add school levels and subjects, making it easy to later categorize added questions under a specific school level or subject.

Phone does not support creating or configuring school levels/subjects under the organization question bank.

1. Go to Organization Question Bank.
2. Select the school/institution.

## 2. Deleting a School Level/Subject

When a school level/subject's question bank is no longer needed at all, it can be deleted. Once deleted, the questions and exam papers in that bank are also permanently deleted; other users will no longer be able to pull questions or papers from that school level/subject when creating a quiz. This cannot be undone once deleted.

Phone does not support deleting.

1. Go to Manage.
2. Delete the school level/subject.

## 3. Enabling/Disabling a School Level/Subject

The school/institution's primary account, and school/institution administrators with "Question Bank Center" permission.

When a school level/subject's question bank in the organization question bank is temporarily not needed, teachers with the relevant permission can choose to disable it. Once disabled, the questions and exam paper data in that bank aren't lost, but can no longer be viewed or used.

Phone does not support enabling or disabling.

1. Enable or Disable the question bank.

## 4. Setting Members and Permissions

The school/institution's primary account, school/institution administrators with "Organization Cloud Drive" permission, and teachers with "Manage" permission for the relevant school level/subject.

Teachers with the relevant permission can add and set members and manage permissions for a school level/subject in the organization question bank. Member and management permissions are as follows:

| Permission | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Manage | Can manage members and add/edit/delete questions and exam papers.<br><br>Questions: view, add, add to My Question Bank, edit, move to, duplicate, forward, delete, add to Question Basket.<br><br>Exam papers: view, add, add to My Question Bank, edit, move to, duplicate, download, forward, delete, add to Question Basket, create new quiz. |
| Operate | Can add/edit/delete questions and exam papers.<br><br>Questions: view, add, edit, delete, add to Question Basket.<br><br>Exam papers: view, add, edit, delete, add to Question Basket, create new quiz. |
| View Only | Can only use questions and exam papers.<br><br>Questions: view, add to Question Basket.<br><br>Exam papers: view, add to Question Basket, create new quiz. |
| Not Visible | Cannot view content under the organization question bank. |

**Super Admin:** refers to a backend school/institution administrator with "Question Bank Center" permission.

Phone does not support setting members and permissions.

1. Go to Member Management.
2. Add a member, or edit member permissions.

## 5. Adding Questions

The school/institution's primary account.

School/institution administrators with "Organization Question Bank" permission.

Teachers with "Manage" or "Operate" permission for the relevant school level/subject.

When adding questions in My Question Bank, users can set up knowledge points or chapters as needed, then add questions under the corresponding knowledge point or chapter. When adding questions on computer or tablet, users can add several question types: single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer, and comprehensive questions (a comprehensive question can contain several sub-questions, supporting single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and short-answer sub-types). When adding questions on phone, only single-choice, multiple-choice, and true/false questions are supported. Once questions are added, users can delete, duplicate, forward, or move them to another subject. The primary account, organization question bank administrators, and teachers with "Manage" permission for the relevant school level/subject can also add questions to My Question Bank.

1. Select the school/institution.

## 6. Adding an Exam Paper

Organization question bank members with "Manage" or "Operate" permission.

Users can add an exam paper in the organization question bank. When creating a paper, you can select questions from both My Question Bank and the organization question bank to add to it, or add new questions directly while creating the paper; on computer, bulk-adding questions is also supported. An already-added paper supports duplicating, forwarding, deleting, moving to a different school level or subject, and creating a quiz; on computer, you can also download the paper locally. The primary account, organization question bank administrators, and teachers with "Manage" permission for the relevant school level/subject can also add questions to My Question Bank.

1. Select a school level/subject and Exam Papers.
