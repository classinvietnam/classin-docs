## Microphone

## I. Microphone Mode

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
While using ClassIn, users may run into the following microphone issues:

A computer user turns on their microphone in the classroom, the microphone icon shows green, but there's no sound when they speak — even after using "Fix Hardware Driver," and even though the microphone works fine in other software.

A computer user turns on their microphone in the classroom, but the microphone toggle keeps automatically switching back to "disabled."

An Android phone or Android tablet user turns on their microphone in the classroom, but there's no sound when they speak.

For the cases above, computer users experiencing this issue are advised to try checking "Legacy Mode 1" or "Legacy Mode 2" under "Microphone Mode" in ClassIn settings. Android phone or tablet users can enable "Microphone Mode" in settings.

### Notes
After a computer user changes "Microphone Mode," ClassIn must be restarted for the change to take effect.

After an Android phone or tablet user enables "Microphone Mode," they should tap the "Start Class" button again to re-enter the classroom.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Classroom Settings.
3. Change Microphone Mode.

## II. Fixing the Microphone

If a user's built-in Windows sound recorder works fine locally, but they run into these microphone issues in a ClassIn classroom, this feature can also help fix the microphone:

No waveform movement while speaking.

Waveform moves while speaking, but there's no sound.

We recommend restarting your computer after fixing the microphone.

Phones and tablets don't have "hardware driver fix" — if you run into this kind of issue on phone or tablet, try restarting the device instead.

Mac computers don't have a "Fix Hardware Driver" feature. If this happens on a Mac, first check whether "Microphone" access is authorized for ClassIn in your Mac's local settings, and grant authorization if not. If it's already authorized and the issue persists, try restarting your computer.

#### Computer (Windows)
1. Go to System Settings.
2. Scroll the right-side scrollbar all the way down.
3. Under Hardware Driver Fix, click Fix.
4. (If a firewall prompt blocks it, click Allow or Yes.) Click Fix Microphone; once it reports the fix is complete, we recommend restarting your computer.

## III. Microphone Gain

When taking class on a phone or tablet, if your own voice sounds too quiet, note that the device's physical volume buttons only adjust speaker volume, not microphone gain. In that case, exit the classroom and enable "Auto Microphone Gain" in the app settings to boost your speaking volume, then re-enter the classroom.

On computer, the classroom supports adjusting microphone volume directly.

#### Phone
1. Turn Auto Microphone Gain on or off.

## IV. Enabling Microphone Boost in Music Mode

If the teacher in a classroom has enabled Music Mode, and a Windows computer user's voice sounds too quiet, that user can enable "Enable Microphone Boost in Music Mode" in classroom settings. Once enabled, ClassIn must be restarted and logged back in for it to take effect.

### Usage Notes
This feature only supports Windows computers.

1. Turn "Enable Microphone Boost in Music Mode" on or off.

## V. Microphone AI Noise Reduction

If a user's surroundings are too noisy while taking class on a phone or tablet, making it hard for others in the classroom to hear them clearly, they can try enabling "AI Noise Reduction" in app settings to improve this.

On computer, the classroom supports enabling background noise reduction.

Once a teacher enables Music Mode in the classroom, noise reduction for classroom users won't take effect, in order to ensure instrument sound comes through properly. Conversely, if Music Mode isn't enabled in the classroom, users' noise reduction will work as expected.

1. Turn AI Noise Reduction on or off.

## VI. Opening Audio a Different Way

For iOS iPhone or iPad users, in certain situations while using ClassIn, and with AI Noise Reduction already enabled, you can try turning on "Open Audio a Different Way." Specific situations include:

**Screen-mirroring issue:** if you mirror the ClassIn screen from your iPhone or iPad to a TV, but the TV can't play ClassIn's audio properly even though other apps on your phone or tablet play sound fine, you can try turning on "Open Audio a Different Way" (with AI Noise Reduction already enabled).

**Microphone issue:** if your voice sounds too quiet in the classroom on iPhone or iPad after enabling AI Noise Reduction, you can try turning on "Open Audio a Different Way" (with AI Noise Reduction already enabled).

"Open Audio a Different Way" can only be enabled on iPhone or iPad after AI Noise Reduction has been enabled in Advanced Settings.

Computers and Android phones/tablets do not have an "Open Audio a Different Way" feature.

#### iPhone
1. Tap Home in the bottom-left corner.
2. First enable AI Noise Reduction, then turn "Open Audio a Different Way" on or off.
