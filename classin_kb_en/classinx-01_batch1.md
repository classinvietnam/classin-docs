## Recording Countdown

## Applies to
The "Backend Management System" primary account.

Sub-accounts with "School Settings" permission.

## When to use this
Academic teachers can change the recording countdown time in the "Backend Management System" based on the teaching teacher's habits. The default recording countdown is 10 minutes — meaning cloud recording automatically starts 10 minutes after the teacher enters the classroom each time. If the teacher wants cloud recording to start the moment they enter the classroom, academic staff can change the countdown to 0 minutes. If the teacher wants more time to prepare and doesn't want recording to start so quickly, academic staff can change the countdown to 20 minutes.

## Usage Notes
The recording countdown time in the "Backend Management System" can be set to: 0 minutes, 1 minute, 3 minutes, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, or 20 minutes.

## Notes
After enabling a new custom classroom configuration, the teacher needs to exit the classroom and re-enter for the new configuration to take effect.

## Related Articles
"Adding Custom Classroom Settings"

"Editing Custom Classroom Settings"

"Adding a Sub-Account in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log into the Backend Management System.
2. Go to School Settings in the left navigation.
3. Go to Classroom Settings above.
4. Create or edit custom classroom settings.
5. Go to Parameter Settings.
6. Under Recording Countdown Settings, change the recording countdown time, then click Confirm.
7. Enable the newly configured custom classroom setting.

## ClassInX Download/Login/Logout/Full Shutdown

# I. Downloading ClassInX Software

## Usage Notes
ClassInX software is an all-in-one education tool for online, offline, and blended teaching, covering three core scenarios, suited to K-12, higher education, tutoring institutions, and other user types — designed for teacher use.

ClassInX software can be downloaded from the official website, www.eeo.cn, and supports Windows 7 and higher — it's not compatible with other operating systems.

## Steps
1. Visit www.eeo.cn in your browser.
2. Click to go to the Download Center.
3. Scroll down using the scrollbar on the right side of the page, and click "Download for Windows" under ClassInX (Large Display Edition).
4. Once downloaded, double-click to open the ClassInX installer.
5. When User Account Control asks whether to allow this app to make changes to your device, click Yes.
6. Select the installation language, then click Confirm.
7. Check the agreement, then click Quick Install or Custom Install.
8. Once installation finishes, the system will automatically open the ClassInX software.

# II. Logging Into a ClassInX Account

Entering a classroom, using cloud drive files, and creating a class within ClassInX all require you to be logged into a ClassIn account.

Once ClassInX is running, users can click or swipe up from the very bottom of the screen to open the dock, then click the avatar on the left to log in.

Login methods include: scanning a QR code with your phone or tablet after logging into ClassIn to log into ClassInX; or entering your ClassIn account (phone number/email) and password to log in.

1. Go to the dock at the very bottom of the classroom.
2. Click the avatar on the left.
3. Scan the QR code, or enter your ClassIn account and password, to log in.

# III. Logging Out of a ClassInX Account

Users can click or swipe up from the very bottom of the screen to open the dock, then click the avatar on the left to log out of their ClassIn account.

1. Click Log Out.
2. Click Confirm Logout.

# IV. Switching ClassInX Accounts

Users can click or swipe up from the very bottom of the screen to open the dock, then click the avatar on the left to switch ClassIn accounts.

1. Click Switch Account.

# V. Fully Closing ClassInX Software

Logging out doesn't mean ClassInX software is closed. To fully close ClassInX software, click "Go to Desktop" in the dock below, then press and hold the ClassInX window in the desktop's taskbar to close the software.

1. Click Go to Desktop.
2. Press and hold the ClassInX window in the taskbar at the bottom of the desktop, then click Close Window.
