## Entering the Classroom for Class

## I. Entering the Classroom for Class

## Applies to
Lead teachers, co-teachers, class session students, auditors, and transfer students.

## Usage Notes
The class session's lead teacher or co-teacher(s) can enter the classroom 20 minutes before the official start time; students can enter 10 minutes before. For example: if class officially starts at 16:00, the teacher can enter as early as 15:40, and students as early as 15:50.

Between when the class session is created and 20 minutes before the official start time, the classroom is a preparation room, not the formal classroom. If the time to enter the formal classroom arrives while the teacher is still in the preparation room, the teacher needs to leave the preparation room and enter the formal classroom before they can interact and teach with students.

Teachers and students can enter the classroom by tapping the "Start Class" button on the ClassIn home page, or by finding the class session in the Schedule and tapping "Start Class."

Note: if a teacher or student exits the classroom during the grace period for running over, they cannot re-enter — there is no entry point available. (For example, if a class session is set to officially start at 16:00 with a 30-minute duration, 16:30–16:50 is the grace period; once the grace period ends, the classroom closes automatically.)

## II. Entering the Classroom on Two Devices

Applies to "lead teachers" and "co-teachers."

## When to use this
If a teacher needs to draw or write on the whiteboard but doesn't have a graphics tablet handy, they can enter the classroom on a computer, then log into ClassIn on an iPad or phone and enter the same classroom to temporarily use it as a writing tablet.

In a class session, a "lead teacher" or "co-teacher" can use two devices for teaching at the same time. Once a teacher enters the classroom, they can choose, in settings, whether the primary or secondary device's camera, microphone, and speaker are in use. Note: only the lead teacher or co-teacher(s) can enter the classroom on two devices — students cannot use this feature.

## Student Trophies

## I. Giving a Trophy to a Student

### Applies to
Lead teacher, co-teacher(s).

### When to use this
When a student performs exceptionally well in class, the teacher can send them a trophy as a reward.

### Notes
Teachers using an Android phone or Android tablet don't have quick-action buttons below the classroom camera view; they can award trophies using the roster tool in the classroom instead.

If "Trophy" doesn't appear among the buttons below a student's camera, go to "Change Buttons Below Student Camera" in the "Backend Management System."

#### Computer/iPhone/iPad

## II. Authorizing a Student

### Applies to
Lead teacher, co-teacher(s).

### When to use this
In the classroom, to keep students actively engaged and improve teaching effectiveness — for example, when a student needs to draw or type on the whiteboard, or trigger a PPT animation — the teacher can individually authorize that student to use teaching tools, so they can complete a learning task or present their work more easily.

### Notes
Teachers using a phone or tablet (Android or HarmonyOS) don't have quick-action buttons below the classroom camera view; they can authorize students to use teaching tools using the roster tool in the classroom instead.

A student can be authorized by the teacher whether they're in a seat on stage or off stage.

Up to 15 students can be authorized in the classroom at the same time. Once that limit is reached, authorizing another student automatically revokes authorization from whichever student was authorized first.

If a student scribbles carelessly on the whiteboard, it may disrupt the teacher's lesson. If this becomes a problem, the teacher can revoke the student's whiteboard drawing permission — i.e. revoke their authorization.

If "Authorize" doesn't appear among the buttons below a student's camera, go to "Change Buttons Below Student Camera" in the "Backend Management System."

There's no separate teaching toolbox on the student side — once a teacher opens certain tools within the toolbox, students can use them at the same time.

### Steps

#### Computer/iPhone/iPad
1. Click the student's camera.
2. Among the buttons below the camera, click Authorize (click again to revoke authorization).

## III. Auto-Authorizing Students Who Join the Stage

In the classroom, to encourage teacher-student interaction, teachers can enable "Auto-Authorize on Stage." Once enabled, as soon as a student joins the stage, they're automatically authorized by the system to use teaching tools — without needing to wait for the teacher to manually authorize them; and when the student leaves the stage, the system automatically revokes their authorization. This significantly improves classroom interaction efficiency, freeing up more time for teaching and discussion. It also saves teachers the time and effort of manually authorizing students, improving teaching efficiency. Once "Auto-Authorize on Stage" is enabled, a gold rotating crown icon appears in the top-right corner of the teacher's camera; once disabled, the icon disappears automatically.

Android phones and Android tablets do not have the "Auto-Authorize Students on Stage" feature.

Up to four buttons can display below the teacher's camera. If "Auto-Authorize Students on Stage" doesn't appear, go to "Change Buttons Below Teacher Camera" in the "Backend Management System."

1. Click your own camera.
2. Among the buttons below the camera, click Auto-Authorize on Stage.

## IV. Authorizing All Students on Stage

### Usage Notes
When the lead teacher wants all students on stage to join in drawing or typing on the whiteboard, triggering PPT animations, or otherwise taking part in classroom interaction, they can use the "Authorize All" feature to make this happen more efficiently.

Android phones and Android tablets do not have the "Authorize All" feature.

Up to four buttons can display below the teacher's camera. If "Authorize All" doesn't appear, go to "Change Buttons Below Teacher Camera" in the "Backend Management System."

1. Among the buttons below the camera, click Authorize All (click again to revoke).

## V. Revoking All Students' Authorization

If a teacher notices during class that a student has been messing with courseware or elements on the whiteboard without permission, and can't tell exactly who's responsible, they can use "Revoke All Authorization." This turns off teaching tool access for every authorized student in the classroom, both on stage and off. This effectively prevents students from disrupting the lesson content without permission.

Android phones and Android tablets do not have the "Revoke All Authorization" feature.

Up to four buttons can display below the teacher's camera. If "Revoke All Authorization" doesn't appear, go to "Change Buttons Below Teacher Camera" in the "Backend Management System."

1. Among the buttons below the camera, click Revoke All Authorization.
