## Local Classroom (Study Room / Whiteboard Editor)

### Applies to
Everyone.

### What Is a Local Classroom?
In ClassIn, the "Local Classroom" refers to the "Whiteboard" feature on the ClassIn main interface. Unlike the "whiteboard" used for teachers and students to write or draw within a class session, the "Whiteboard" on the main interface is actually a local classroom for getting familiar with ClassIn's features and controls — especially useful for teachers and students using ClassIn for the first time. Using this feature can significantly improve classroom efficiency and teaching quality.

For teachers already skilled with ClassIn, they can go into the "Whiteboard" on the main interface anytime to create whiteboard content and write lesson plans. In addition, the "Whiteboard" on the main interface also supports local recording, giving teachers more convenience for teaching prep and practice.

### When to Use This
**Getting a full picture of classroom features:** teachers and students can go into the "Whiteboard" on the ClassIn main interface anytime to learn and get familiar with the various classroom features.

**Better class prep:** teachers can go into the "Whiteboard" on the ClassIn main interface ahead of time to learn how to use the tools and features they'll need in class. Teachers can prepare lesson plans, create whiteboard content, and edit courseware, getting fully ready for class in advance, ensuring the class content flows smoothly and continuously.

**More classroom interaction:** for teachers already skilled with ClassIn, the "Whiteboard" on the main interface becomes an efficient teaching aid. They can create polished whiteboard content, presenting the lesson's key points through a mix of text and images, which grabs students' attention and improves teaching outcomes.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Click Whiteboard to enter the local whiteboard.

#### Phone
1. Click Growth.
2. Go to Whiteboard.

## Teacher Lesson Prep

# How Do Teachers Prepare Lessons?

The "Prep Room" is a lesson preparation feature ClassIn provides for teachers, for creating courseware and whiteboard content before class officially starts. Once a class session is created, the lead teacher and co-teacher(s) can enter the prep room to prepare, starting 20 minutes before class starts. Only the lead teacher and co-teacher(s) can access the prep room — no one else can enter.

In the prep room, teachers can prepare and edit courseware and whiteboard content in advance, and can exit and re-enter the prep room anytime to keep working on their teaching content. The prep room is an independent virtual classroom, so teachers won't run into other teachers there. Once there's less than 20 minutes left before class officially starts, the lead teacher using a computer can go directly from the prep room into the formal classroom.

When the lead teacher prepares on computer, the whiteboard content in the prep room is automatically saved once the lead teacher exits, and they can choose to open the prep content once they enter the formal classroom. When the lead teacher prepares on phone or tablet, when exiting the classroom they can choose to save the whiteboard's content to the cloud drive; once in the formal classroom, if they need to open that prep content, they can find and open it from the cloud drive.

Regardless of which device a co-teacher uses to prepare, exiting the prep room does not automatically save the prep content — when exiting, they're prompted whether to save the whiteboard content to the cloud drive.

# Applies To
"Lead teacher" and "co-teacher."

# Notes
Within 20 minutes of the official class start time, a lead teacher preparing on phone or tablet cannot automatically enter the formal classroom — the teacher needs to manually exit the prep room and re-enter the formal classroom.

# Steps

## Step 1: Enter the Prep Room

#### Computer
1. Go to Schedule in the left navigation.
2. Select the class date.
3. Enter the prep classroom.

## Step 2: Create Whiteboard Content

### (1) Opening Teaching Courseware

The teacher opens already-uploaded courseware, or directly drags courseware from their local computer into the classroom. If the courseware contains animation effects or video, we recommend testing it in advance to make sure it plays properly. In the classroom, you can use the "Click Tool" to operate it.

### (2) Pen

Teachers can mark up, write, and draw on the whiteboard or courseware. While drawing, you can choose to draw several shapes, including a perfect circle, ellipse, square, rectangle, irregular triangle, equilateral triangle, straight line, and curve. This variety of shape options makes drawing more flexible and versatile, meeting different drawing needs. Besides choosing a shape, the Pen tool also lets teachers freely adjust the pen's color and thickness, achieving more professional, refined drawing effects. Teachers can choose a suitable color as needed — whether bright or deep tones — to give the drawing more artistic character. In addition, teachers can choose solid or dashed lines for more professional, delicate drawing effects.

### (3) Text Entry

Teachers can use the "Text" tool to type any text, letters, or numbers onto the whiteboard or courseware. You can set font color and size before typing.

### (4) Select/Move (Secondary Editing)

Teachers can use this tool to single-select, multi-select, and move various whiteboard elements — images, shapes, text, and so on. Once you select an element on the whiteboard, a "secondary editing" bar pops up below it. Teachers can use the shortcuts in this bar to modify and adjust the selected element.

### (5) Eraser

Teachers can use the eraser tool to erase content drawn or written with the Pen tool on the whiteboard, and it also includes a "one-click clear" feature — if you encounter whiteboard elements that can't be erased normally, use "one-click clear" to wipe the entire whiteboard clean.

### (6) Saving the Whiteboard

Teachers can use this tool during or after lesson prep to save the whiteboard's content locally on their computer or to the cloud drive.

## Step 3: Going from the Prep Room into the Formal Classroom

When a lead teacher prepares on computer, once there's less than 20 minutes left before official class start, the prep room shows a prompt asking whether to enter the formal classroom right away — clicking "Enter Now" jumps to the formal classroom and automatically loads the prep content; if the lead teacher exits the prep room and then enters the formal classroom, the formal classroom shows a prompt asking whether to load the prep content — click "Yes" to load it.

When a teacher prepares on phone or tablet, once there's less than 20 minutes left before official class start, the prep room shows a notification that they can now enter the formal classroom; the teacher then needs to manually exit the prep room and enter the formal classroom, and when exiting can choose whether to save the prep room's whiteboard content to the cloud drive.
