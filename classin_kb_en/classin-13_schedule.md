## Personal Calendar Event

## I. Creating a New Calendar Event

## Applies to
Everyone.

## When to use this
Students can use this to plan personal study tasks, mark important academic milestones, and record activities outside of class; teachers can use it to schedule personalized teaching work, note lesson-prep and research items, and record one-on-one tutoring tasks. Creating a personal calendar event in Schedule helps both teachers and students efficiently manage their own time without conflicting with the established teaching schedule.

## Usage Notes
Users on computer or tablet can create a new calendar event in Schedule. An event supports entering a subject, setting the time, whether it's all-day, whether it repeats (no repeat, repeats daily, or repeats weekly), whether to add it to To-Dos, and adding a description. Once created, the event shows up on your personal calendar within Schedule.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to Schedule in the left navigation.
2. Click "+" in the top-right corner.
3. Create the new event.

## II. Editing a Calendar Event

Users on computer or tablet can edit an event whose end time hasn't passed yet.

Every field in the event's details supports editing.

For a repeating event, you can choose the scope of your edit: "This Event" or "This Event and Following Events."

## Notes
Phone only supports viewing an event.

1. Find and click the event.
2. Click Edit.

## III. Deleting a Calendar Event

Users on computer or tablet can delete an event; a deleted event cannot be recovered — proceed with caution.

For a repeating event, you can choose the scope of deletion: "This Event," "This Event and Following Events," or "All Events."

## Schedule

## I. What Is "Schedule"?

Schedule is a detailed calendar table recording the courses a given school, grade, or class needs. It lists the weekly course arrangement, class times, teaching teachers, and class locations, making it easy for students and teachers to understand and plan learning content and progress. Schedule is an important teaching tool, and also part of a school's overall teaching management.

## II. Viewing a Class Session/Activity/Calendar Event

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
In Schedule, users can find and enter class sessions, activities, and calendar events based on their role and the date, or click a date to view class sessions, activities, and personal calendar events they have permission to take part in on that date. Schedule also supports a calendar view — in calendar view, activities are shown below the date, and class sessions and calendar events are shown within the time grid.

A class session's lead teacher, co-teacher(s), and students can enter the classroom for class from Schedule; the lead teacher and co-teacher(s) can also enter the class session's prep room from here. After the class session ends, the lead teacher, co-teacher(s), and students can view the session's playback video, whiteboard courseware, teaching report, or learning report.

Only published activities with a due date can show up in Schedule. If there's an activity that needs to be completed, the corresponding teacher and students can also view and enter that activity's details page.

If there's a calendar event, it can also be viewed or entered from here.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to Schedule in the left navigation.
2. Select the date.
3. View or enter the class session, activity, or calendar event.

#### Phone
1. Go to Schedule in the bottom navigation.

## III. Watching the "Class Playback Video" in Schedule

The class session's lead teacher, co-teacher(s), and students.

When creating the class session, if you checked cloud recording (Record ClassIn Classroom / Record On-Site), and the lead teacher's recording during the class session went normally, then about half an hour after class it will automatically be turned into a "playback video." The class session's lead teacher, co-teacher(s), and students can all watch the "playback video" in "Schedule." Class teachers and the class session's students can also view this class session's "playback video" in the class.

1. View the playback video.
2. Select the date.

## IV. Viewing "Whiteboard Courseware" in Schedule

If whiteboard courseware retention is enabled at www.eeo.cn, and the conditions for generating "whiteboard courseware" are met in the formal classroom, then after class ends, the class session's lead teacher, co-teacher(s), and students can view this session's "whiteboard courseware" in Schedule. Class teachers and the class session's students can also view this class session's "whiteboard courseware" in the class.

1. View the whiteboard courseware.

## V. Viewing the "Teaching Report" in Schedule

The class session's lead teacher and co-teacher(s).

## Usage Notes
Provided the formal classroom's lead teacher or co-teacher(s) attended normally, a "teaching report" for that session's teacher is generated half an hour after class ends. The session's lead teacher and co-teacher(s) can view it in Schedule, and can also view it in the class; the head teacher, however, can only view the "teaching report" in the class. Once you're in the teaching report, you can also view students' learning reports.

1. View the teaching report.

## VI. Viewing the "Learning Report" in Schedule

Class session students.

Half an hour after the formal classroom ends, a "learning report" is generated for that session's students; the session's students can view it in Schedule, and can also view the "learning report" in the class. If the class session enabled the feature allowing students to view each other's learning reports, students can view classmates' "learning reports" in the class session's info after class.

1. View the learning report.
