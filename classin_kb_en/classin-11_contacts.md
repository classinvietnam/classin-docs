## Friend Management

## I. How to Add a Friend

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
Students can add each other as friends to communicate privately, share learning experiences, form study partnerships, motivate each other, and support each other's success. Students can also start a temporary classroom with a friend to work through learning questions and challenges together.

Students and teachers can also add each other as friends. Teachers can add a student as a friend to send them a notice or content privately; students can also ask a teacher for help with a personal learning problem and get professional guidance and advice, further improving their learning outcomes and efficiency. This kind of communication and interaction helps build a closer connection between students and teachers, fostering a good teacher-student relationship.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Click the "+" icon at the top.
2. Select Add Friend.
3. Enter a phone number or email to search for a friend, or share your own QR code or In passcode with a friend.

#### Phone
1. Go to Home.
2. Click the "+" icon in the top-right corner.
3. Add a friend via search, phone contacts, QR code scan, In passcode, WeChat invite, and so on.

## II. Accepting or Declining a Friend Request from Someone Else

When another user sends you a friend request, you can handle it under "New Friends" in the contacts list. You have two options: "Accept" or "Decline."

1. Go to Messages in the left navigation.
2. Click Contacts.
3. Go to New Friends.
4. Click the friend.
5. Accept or Decline the friend request.

Go to Messages.
Go to Contacts.
Go to New Contacts.
Tap the user who sent the friend request.
Select Accept or Decline.

## III. Friend Nicknames/Notes

### When to use this
In the ClassIn app, if a friend's display name isn't their real name, or if you have too many friends to keep track of, you can add a note for them as needed, making your friends list easier to manage.

1. Open Contacts.
2. Go to Friends.
3. Select the friend.
4. Edit the friend's note.

Set a note for the friend.

## V. Friend Temporary Classroom

The temporary classroom feature between friends is designed for two friends to create a temporary classroom at a specific time to study, discuss, and share knowledge together. This kind of temporary classroom scenario is useful in many situations.

### Notes
The temporary classroom doesn't have a "class notes" feature.

1. Click Send Message.
2. Click Temporary Classroom near the input box.

Click Start Temporary Classroom.

## VI. Muting Friend Messages

Once you enable "Mute Friend Messages," when a friend you've muted sends you a message, ClassIn won't send you any message notification for it.

1. Turn "Mute Messages" on or off.

Go to Friend Settings.
Turn "Mute Messages" on/off.

## VII. Pinning a Friend Chat

Friend chats support pinning, which keeps a friend's chat permanently fixed at the top of your message list, so it doesn't get buried by other messages, making it easy to find the conversation quickly.

1. Click Friend Settings.
2. Turn "Pin Friend Chat" on/off.
