## Resetting Your Password

### When to use this
If you forget your ClassIn login password, you can use the "Forgot Password" feature in ClassIn to reset it.

### Notes
There is a combined daily limit of 10 SMS, email, and voice verification codes. Once you reach this limit, you won't be able to request any more codes that day.

### Steps

**Computer/tablet**
1. Open ClassIn.
2. Click Forgot Password.
3. Enter your phone number or email, click Get Verification Code, and set a new password.

## Registering an Account

### Prerequisites
Before registering for ClassIn, make sure you have a phone number or email address that can receive verification codes normally, so you can receive the registration code.

### Usage Notes
When registering for ClassIn, if you don't receive the SMS verification code, wait 60 seconds, then tap to request a "voice verification code."

Non-Chinese phone numbers can also be used to register a ClassIn account — just make sure to select the correct country code during registration. For example, for a US phone number, select "+1."

Voice verification codes are only supported for mainland China phone numbers (with the +86 country code).

Users in mainland China who register with an email address will need to verify with a phone number as well.

There is a combined daily limit of 10 SMS, email, and voice verification codes. Once you reach this limit, you won't be able to request any more codes that day.

### Steps

**Computer/tablet**
1. Open ClassIn.
2. Click Sign Up Now.
3. Enter your phone number or email, click Get Verification Code, and set a login password.

## II. One-Tap Login on Phone

### Prerequisites
Your phone must have an active, working SIM card to use "One-Tap Login with This Number."

### Notes
This feature is not available for computer or tablet users.

Tap "One-Tap Login with This Number" to log in to ClassIn directly.

## III. QR Code Login

### Usage Notes
First, install and log in to ClassIn on your phone or tablet, or make sure ClassIn is already linked to your WeChat account. Then open ClassIn on your computer and use the "scan" feature in the ClassIn or WeChat app on your phone or tablet to scan the QR code shown on the ClassIn login page on your computer, to complete login.

1. On your computer, open ClassIn and click QR Code Login.
2. On your phone/tablet, open ClassIn or WeChat and log in.
3. Tap the "+" icon in the top-right corner of ClassIn or WeChat on your phone/tablet, and select Scan.
4. Scan the QR code shown in ClassIn on your computer.

## IV. Verification Code Login

Make sure your phone or email can receive SMS or emails normally before using the verification code login feature.

There is a combined daily limit of 10 SMS, email, and voice verification codes. Once you reach this limit, you won't be able to request any more codes that day.

1. Open ClassIn.
2. Tap Other Login Methods.
3. Tap Verification Code Login.
4. Enter your phone number or email, then request a verification code.

![](https://cofile.eeo.cn/res-store%2F71953d3e3359fed4d7a58fea6948377b39aac2c29b4695e6a440b189f291f8d3_1032011?q-sign-algorithm=sha1&q-ak=AKIDJQGsEOK2TfbFdEZifMnxrWx85mpdHj6H&q-sign-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-key-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-header-list=host&q-url-param-list=&q-signature=12f6209f2658558664bad3afbd1ba59e6e433a43)

## V. WeChat or Apple Login

Once you've linked ClassIn to your WeChat or Apple ID on your phone or tablet, you can log in with WeChat or Apple.

1. Open ClassIn on your phone or tablet, and tap Other Login Methods.
2. Tap WeChat Login or Apple Login.
3. Complete the linking verification to log in.
