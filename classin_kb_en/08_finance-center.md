## Top-Up

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "Finance Center" permission

### Usage Notes
ClassIn offers two top-up methods: online payment and bank wire transfer. "Online payment" offers four payment options: WeChat Pay, Alipay, PayPal, and Stripe. The top-up amount ranges from ¥2,000 to ¥1,000,000. For fast crediting, use online payment. For bank wire transfer details, go to Backend Management System → Finance Overview → Top-Up → Bank Wire Transfer to view the remittance information.

### Related Articles
"Fee Deduction Rules"
"Issuing Invoices"
"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to Finance Overview in the left navigation.
3. Click Top-Up in the upper-right corner.

## Invoice Management

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "Finance Center" permission

### Usage Notes
On the "Invoice Management" page, teaching staff can filter invoices by: request date (the date the invoice was requested), invoice type (regular invoice or special VAT invoice), invoice status (e.g. issuing, needs revision, issued, closed), amount range (the amount the invoice was issued for), and by searching the application number, company/individual name, or tax ID. Teaching staff can issue invoices for ClassIn top-ups from the Backend Management System. While an invoice is being processed, staff can cancel the request at any time, and issued invoices can be downloaded locally.

## Storage Consumption Statistics

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "Finance Center" permission

When teaching staff want to view storage consumption statistics for the Backend Management System over a given period, they can download them as a spreadsheet from the "Statistics & Downloads" module. ## Fee Deduction Rules

### What are fee deduction rules?
Fee deduction rules are the rules by which a school or organization is charged for specific features while using ClassIn. These rules are set by ClassIn and vary depending on the type of service and how it is used.

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "Finance Center" permission

### Usage Notes
ClassIn's billing is based on a prepaid-balance model. A school or organization tops up its ClassIn Backend Management System account in advance, and as users use various ClassIn features, the corresponding fees are deducted from the school's/organization's ClassIn account. Note that all fees are deducted from the school's/organization's ClassIn account balance — ClassIn never charges individual teachers or students directly.

| Item | Fee Deduction Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Class Session (Lesson) | Total charge for a class session = student charge + co-teacher charge (skip if none) + recording charge (skip if none) |
| Students | Student charge formula: student charge = hourly rate per on-stage seat × number of validly attending students and auditors × lesson duration<br><br>(1) Hourly rate per on-stage seat: calculated based on the number of on-stage seats set when the lesson was created. The specific rate can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Validly attending students and auditors: a student or auditor must accumulate at least 10 minutes in the classroom to be counted as validly attending.<br><br>(3) Lesson duration: calculated based on the duration set when the lesson was created. If the lesson ends early, billing is based on the actual class time. |
| Co-Teachers | Co-teacher charge formula: actual co-teacher charge = co-teacher rate × lesson duration<br><br>(1) Co-teacher rate: the specific rate can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend. The co-teacher rate varies depending on the "on-stage seat count" tier of the lesson. (Billing for a co-teacher starts once their cumulative time in the classroom reaches 10 minutes; under 10 minutes incurs no charge.)<br><br>(2) Lesson duration: calculated based on the duration set when the lesson was created. If the lesson ends early, billing is based on the actual class time. (Note: the minimum billing unit is half an hour; any duration under half an hour is billed as half an hour.) |
| Recording | Recording charge formula: actual recording charge = hourly recording rate × recording duration<br><br>(1) Hourly recording rate: can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Recording duration: based on the actual duration of the generated playback video (minimum unit is one minute; any duration under one minute is billed as one minute). If the "playback video duration" is shorter than the "billable lesson duration," billing is based on the playback video duration. If the "playback video duration" is longer than the "billable lesson duration," billing is based on the billable lesson duration. |
| Web Live Stream | Web live stream traffic charge formula: actual web live stream traffic charge = rate per unit of traffic × live stream traffic<br><br>(1) Traffic rate: the rate per GB can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Web live stream traffic: the first 100MB of live stream traffic per lesson is free. |
| Web Playback | Web playback traffic charge formula: actual web playback traffic charge = rate per unit of traffic × playback traffic<br><br>(1) Traffic rate: the rate per GB can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Web playback traffic: the backend traffic generated when users watch the web playback, and the backend traffic generated when downloading recorded lesson videos.<br><br>(3) Note: playback traffic for the previous month is billed on the 1st of each month. |
| Storage Fees | Storage charge formula: actual storage charge = (lesson resources + course attachments + class files + paid organization cloud drive + teaching plans − storage cap) × storage rate<br><br>(1) Storage is billed on the 1st of each month. Actual storage usage within the organization's storage cap is not billed; usage beyond the cap is billed at the storage rate. Actual charges are subject to the contract terms.<br><br>(2) Lesson resources include billable recorded class videos, billable monitoring images, and whiteboard courseware. System-generated recorded videos and monitoring images are free for the first 3 months; if kept in storage beyond 3 months, storage fees apply. If deleted within 3 months, no fee is charged afterward. Uploaded lesson videos are not eligible for the 3-month free period; storage fees are calculated at the end of the current month.<br><br>(3) Monitoring image storage is calculated at 200KB per image; 1GB (approximately 5,242 images) is one billing unit, and any amount under 1GB is not billed.<br><br>(4) Course attachments are not eligible for the 3-month free period; storage fees are calculated at the end of the current month.<br><br>(5) Class files are not eligible for the 3-month free period; storage fees are calculated at the end of the current month.<br><br>(6) The organization cloud drive is not eligible for the 3-month free period; storage fees are calculated at the end of the current month.<br><br>(7) Teaching plans are not eligible for the 3-month free period; storage fees are calculated at the end of the current month. |
| SMS Notifications | SMS charge formula: actual SMS charge = mainland China SMS rate × number of mainland China SMS messages sent + Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan and international SMS rate × number of Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan and international SMS messages sent<br><br>(1) Mainland China SMS rate: can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan and international SMS rate: can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(3) Number of SMS messages sent: messages of 70 characters or fewer count as one message; messages over 70 characters are split into segments of 67 characters each (no distinction between Chinese and English — every character, letter, digit, space, or symbol counts as one character). |
| Voice Calls | Voice call charge formula: actual voice call charge = mainland China voice call rate × actual mainland China call minutes<br><br>(1) Mainland China voice call rate: can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Actual mainland China call minutes: any call under 1 minute is billed as 1 minute. |
| Smart Homeroom Teacher | (1) The annual fee for the "Smart Homeroom Teacher" feature can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Once "Smart Homeroom Teacher" is enabled, the system immediately deducts one year's activation fee from your account.<br><br>(3) If you do not disable this feature by 23:59:00 on the last day before renewal, the system will automatically renew it for another year. If you do not wish to continue using this feature, please disable it before 23:59:00 on the last day.<br><br>(4) Note: using "Smart Homeroom Teacher" incurs SMS and voice call charges, which are billed separately based on actual usage. See the "Actual SMS Charge" and "Actual Voice Call Charge" sections for details. |
| Super Customer Service | Super Customer Service charge formula: Super Customer Service charge = mainland China callback rate × actual mainland China call minutes<br><br>(1) Mainland China callback rate: can be viewed under "Fee Deduction Rules" in the upper right of the "Finance Overview" page in the backend.<br><br>(2) Actual mainland China call minutes: the caller and the callee are each billed separately based on their own actual call duration; any call under 1 minute is billed as 1 minute. |

## Order Details

### Applies to
- Primary account of the Backend Management System
- Sub-accounts with the "Finance Center" permission

### Usage Notes
Teaching staff can view and download all order information in the "Financial Details" module of the Backend Management System. To make filtering and analysis easier, the system provides several filter options:

- **Transaction time:** filter orders by a specified date range.
- **Direction of funds:** view income orders or expense orders.
- **Order type:** (Income orders: include service-tier top-up, top-up, gift, service-fault compensation, scheduling-fault compensation, account transfer-in, and other types.) (Expense orders: include account transfer-out, lesson consumption, recording, live stream traffic, playback traffic, storage, SMS, voice calls, Super Customer Service, ClassIn service fee, upgraded trial user, account refund, gift clawback, annual offset deduction, manual finance deduction, monthly service fee, and other types.)
- **Order status:** view only successful or only failed transactions.
- **Amount range:** set an income or expense amount range to filter orders.
- **Search:** search precisely by order number or order content.

Teaching staff can use the above filters to precisely narrow down order information before downloading. The downloaded order data includes: transaction date range, number of orders, expense/income amount, order number, order time, transaction time, order type, order content, and order status.

Through the "Financial Details" module, teaching staff can efficiently manage and analyze all order information on the platform.

"Adding Sub-accounts in the Backend Management System"

### Steps
1. Log in to the Backend Management System.
2. Go to Financial Details in the left navigation.
3. Filter orders to view them, or click Download Spreadsheet on the right.
