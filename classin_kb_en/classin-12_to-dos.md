## To-Dos

## I. What Is "To-Dos"?

The "To-Dos" feature is designed to help users efficiently manage and track unfinished learning and teaching activities. By bringing together every class activity a user is part of into one place, the To-Dos module makes it easier to check progress and complete tasks. It also reminds users about tasks that are about to come due, making sure important tasks don't get missed. Whether you're a student or a teacher, To-Dos can significantly improve how efficiently you manage tasks.

## II. Getting to a Task from To-Dos

### Applies to
Teachers and students with an activity, announcement, or schedule item.

### Usage Notes
When a teacher publishes a learning activity in a class — such as a class session, assignment, quiz, notice, discussion, answer sheet, check-in, recorded course, or learning materials — students can find these pending tasks in their own To-Dos. Tapping the name of a to-do takes the student to the corresponding page to complete the task. Once a student completes a task, the teacher who created the activity can check the student's progress in To-Dos and grade it.

Users can also view and confirm their personal schedule and class announcements in To-Dos.

Once inside "To-Dos," users can view three sections: "Pending," "Upcoming," and "Processed."

**Pending:** shows every unfinished task, grouped by past due, due within a day, due within a week, due after a week, and no due date. Users can filter by role, class, or to-do type to quickly find a task, then tap its name to go to that item and view/complete it.

**Upcoming:** shows to-dos that aren't flagged red — tasks the teacher or student doesn't need to handle yet, or that haven't started — grouped the same way (past due, due within a day, due within a week, due after a week, no due date), making it easy for users to see what's coming up and plan their time. Users can filter by role, class, or to-do type to quickly find a task, then tap its name to go to that item and view/complete it.

**Processed:** tasks that are completed, or manually marked/cleared from their current category, are automatically moved here.

### Notes
A class session only appears under "Pending" once its scheduled class time arrives — before that, it shows under "Upcoming."

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to To-Dos in the left navigation.
2. Select Pending / Upcoming / Processed.
3. Search (by activity name) or filter your to-dos.
4. Click the button on the right to go to the corresponding to-do task page.

#### Phone
1. Go to To-Dos.
2. Tap to go to the corresponding to-do task page.

## III. Marking or Clearing the Current Category

Users can manually mark or clear to-do tasks under "Pending" and "Upcoming" that they no longer need to complete, to better manage their task list. Tasks manually marked/cleared from the current category are automatically moved to "Processed."

Class sessions do not support marking.

1. Select Pending / Upcoming.
2. Mark, or Clear Current Category.

## IV. Unmarking a Processed Item

If you accidentally mark a task as processed by mistake, you can find that task under "Processed" and unmark it. Once unmarked, the task can be found again under "Pending" or "Upcoming," or you can jump directly to the task page from "Processed" to keep working on it.

Class sessions do not support unmarking.

1. Go to Processed.
2. Click the checkmark to the left of the title to unmark it, or click on the right to go to the corresponding to-do task page.
