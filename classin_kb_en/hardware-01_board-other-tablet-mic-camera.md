## [Other] Hardware Usage Issues

# 1. Re-Pairing the Page-Turner Remote and Receiver

**Symptom:** the page-turner remote isn't working and needs to be re-paired with its receiver.

**Solution:** to pair, press and hold Button 1, then quickly press Button 2 five times in a row; the indicator light will start blinking, entering pairing mode. Release Button 1 to finish pairing.

# 2. The Page-Turner Remote's Range Is Too Short

**Symptom:** the page-turner remote's range is too short, making it inconvenient to use.

**Solution:** plug the receiver into a USB port on the side of the large display, as close as possible, to ensure there's nothing blocking the receiver.

## [Handwriting Tablet] Hardware Usage Issues

# 1. Writing Behaves Abnormally

**Symptom:** the handwriting tablet doesn't write correctly.

**Solution:** try any of the following methods to resolve this.

**Confirm the driver is installed:** make sure the ClassInTablet driver is correctly installed. Driver download: https://www.eeo.cn/cn/download (once installed, please make sure the driver keeps running minimized in the background — don't close it.)

**Check for driver conflicts:** confirm no other handwriting tablet drivers are installed on your computer, such as Gaomon or Wacom. If any are installed, please uninstall them to avoid conflicts.

**Check the cable:** please use the original cable or a compatible Huawei phone cable to connect (a charging-only cable won't work).

# 2. Replacing the Stylus Nib

**Symptom:** the stylus's nib is significantly worn down, affecting normal use, and needs to be replaced.

**Solution:** try the method shown in the image below to resolve this.

# 3. Driver Shows "Not Connected"

**Symptom:** the handwriting tablet's driver shows "not connected."

Try connecting through a different USB port.

Try using a different USB cable to connect.

If you're on a Mac, try connecting through a different USB hub/dock.

# 4. Laggy Writing

**Symptom:** the handwriting tablet lags while writing.

**Solution:** please download and install 360 Driver Master, and use it to update your graphics driver, to improve the handwriting tablet's writing experience.

# 5. Writing Registers Without Touching the Tablet

**Symptom:** the stylus registers writing even without touching the tablet.

**Solution:** this is usually caused by a faulty stylus — we recommend contacting ClassIn after-sales support to replace it with a new one.

## [Wireless Microphone] Hardware Usage Issues

# 1. The Charging Case Won't Charge (Mammoth LARK150)

**Symptom:** the Mammoth LARK150 wireless microphone's ("little bee") charging case won't charge.

**Solution:** follow these steps to reset the device.

Connect the charging case to a charger.

Using a pin, press and hold the reset pinhole on the charging case, microphone, and receiver for 5 seconds each.

Remove the microphone and receiver from the charging case, and charge the case alone for 30 minutes.

After 30 minutes, put the microphone and receiver back into the charging case — you should now see the battery level displayed, and the charging case should charge normally again.

# 2. How Do I Wire It Up? (Mammoth LARK150, M1, Rode)

**Symptom:** microphone cable connection issue.

**Solution:** follow these steps.

**Connecting a wireless microphone to a large display all-in-one device (using a TRS-to-TRS cable):**

One end connects to the wireless microphone ("little bee").

The other end connects to the red microphone audio jack on the large display's OPS host.

**Connecting a wireless microphone to a laptop (using a TRS-to-TRRS cable):**

The TRS end connects to the wireless microphone ("little bee").

The TRRS end connects to the laptop's 3.5mm audio jack.

**Connecting a wireless microphone to a phone or tablet (using a TRS-to-USB-C cable):**

The other end connects to the phone or tablet's USB-C port.

**Connecting a wireless microphone to a USB sound card (using a TRS-to-TRS cable):**

The other end connects to the red microphone audio jack on the USB sound card.

# 4. Sound Is Hard to Hear Clearly (Mammoth LARK150, Mammoth M1, Rode)

**Symptom:** when using the Mammoth LARK150, Mammoth M1, or Rode wireless microphone ("little bee"), the sound is too quiet and hard to hear clearly.

**Solution:** try any of the following methods to resolve this.

**Adjust the device's own volume:** first, check and raise the volume setting on the wireless microphone device itself. Make sure the device volume is at an appropriate level.

**Adjust the ClassIn classroom microphone volume:** once in the ClassIn classroom, find the microphone volume setting and raise it appropriately, to make sure the sound is clearly audible.

**Turn off ClassIn classroom background noise reduction:** in the ClassIn classroom, disable background noise reduction. This can help boost the microphone's volume and audio quality, making the sound clearer.

# 5. Battery Life

**Symptom:** wanting to know the battery life of the Mammoth M1's transmitter, receiver, and charging case.

**Answer:**

**Mammoth M1 wireless microphone:**

Both the transmitter and receiver can run continuously for 8 hours on a full charge.

The included charging case can fully recharge the transmitter and receiver twice each, supporting extended use.

**Mammoth LARK150 wireless microphone:**

The transmitter runs continuously for 4 hours on a full charge.

The receiver runs continuously for 7 hours on a full charge.

The charging case, once fully charged, can recharge two transmitters and one receiver 2.5 times.

Note: as with a phone, battery life may shorten in cold environments. In addition, different usage distances can also affect the transmitter's and receiver's battery life.

# 6. Three Working Modes (Mammoth LARK150)

Press and hold the button on the left side of the Mammoth LARK150 receiver to cycle through three working modes: mono mode, stereo mode, and safety-track mode.

# 7. What Do the Three Working Modes Do? (Mammoth LARK150)

The Mammoth LARK150 has three working modes, each with the following effect:

**Stereo mode:** when audio is captured using two microphones (MIC1 and MIC2), the receiver outputs MIC1's audio to the left channel and MIC2's audio to the right channel. This way, users can clearly distinguish the sound captured by each of the two microphones.

**Mono mode:** in this mode, the audio captured by both microphones (MIC1 and MIC2) is merged, and the receiver outputs the merged audio to both the left and right channels. This way, the left and right channel content is identical, forming mono audio.

**Safety-track mode:** in this mode, the receiver merges the audio from MIC1 and MIC2, but the left and right channel volumes differ. The left channel plays at normal volume, while the right channel plays 6dB quieter than the left. This provides a backup audio track during recording, protecting against audio distortion caused by excessive volume, ensuring both audio quality and user privacy are protected.

# 8. What's the Point of Safety-Track Mode?

During live recording, you may run into a sudden spike in volume, and audio that's too loud can distort or ruin the recording file. To handle this, you can use the Mammoth LARK150's safety-track mode. This mode generates two-channel audio while recording — the left channel records audio at normal volume, while the right channel records audio at a lower volume. This way, even if the left channel's audio clips or distorts, the right channel's audio can still serve as a backup, ensuring your recording material isn't rendered completely unusable.

# 9. Pairing the Transmitter and Receiver

**Enter transmitter pairing mode:** press and hold the yellow button on the transmitter for about 3 seconds, until the blue indicator light blinks rapidly.

**Enter receiver pairing mode:** press and hold the "+" button on the receiver for about 3 seconds, until the blue indicator light blinks rapidly.

**Wait for pairing to finish:** once the transmitter's and receiver's blue indicator lights turn solid, pairing has succeeded.

**Mammoth LARK150 wireless microphone:**

To pair the Mammoth LARK150, simply place the transmitter and receiver into the charging case and power it on — they'll pair automatically, with no extra steps needed.

# 10. How to Lock/Unlock the Frequency on a WP1 Wireless Microphone

**Symptom:** needing to change the frequency lock status on a WP1 microphone.

**Answer:** please follow these steps.

**Locking the handheld mic:**

Once the handheld mic and receiver are paired, on the handheld unit, press and release "Page Up" + "Page Down" together — the screen will show the channel blinking; then press and release "Volume Up" to complete the operation. Once locked successfully, a small lock icon appears at the top of the screen.

**Unlocking the handheld mic:**

Press and release "Page Up" + "Page Down" together — the screen will show the channel blinking; then press and release "Volume Down" to complete the operation.

**Locking the receiver:**

Once the receiver automatically pairs with the handheld mic, flip all three "channel" DIP switches on the receiver to the ON position to complete the lock.

**Unlocking the receiver:**

Adjust the receiver's "channel" DIP switches so that not all three are in the ON position.

Note: frequency lock settings require both the handheld mic and receiver to be set to frequency lock mode — otherwise they cannot link and pair.

## [PTZ Camera] Hardware Usage Issues

# 1. Blurry Camera Image

**Symptom:** once the computer is connected to the camera, the displayed camera image is blurry.

**Solution:** try any of the following methods to resolve this.

Check whether the lens has dust or another obstruction, and clean it if so.

Adjust the focus using the remote control or the camera software.

Use the remote control or the camera's companion software to adjust the camera's focus. We recommend not checking image sharpness through the ClassIn whiteboard interface, since the image may be compressed there. Please use ClassIn's Auxiliary Camera feature, or Windows' built-in Camera app, to check the camera's true sharpness.

Install the iSmart CMS software on your computer, and adjust image sharpness using the focus feature (Focus +/-).

# 2. Camera Image Has Ripple/Flicker Artifacts

**Symptom:** once a computer or large display all-in-one device is connected to the camera, the displayed camera image shows a rippling/flickering pattern.

**Using the remote control:**

Go into the menu via the remote.

Select "Image" > "Exposure" > "Anti-Flicker" in sequence.

Change the anti-flicker parameter to 50Hz or 60Hz.

**Regarding the display type:**

If the large display isn't a ClassInX-brand display, and is a dot-matrix screen or uses PWM (pulse-width modulation) dimming, the ripple/flicker effect may not be fully eliminated.

Try increasing screen brightness or adjusting the camera's exposure settings to improve the ripple/flicker effect.

# 3. Disabling Person Tracking (CAM35/CAM36)

**Symptom:** once the computer or large display all-in-one device is connected to the camera, you need to disable person tracking.

Install the iSmart CMS software on your computer, and make sure the camera and computer are connected to the same local network.

Open iSmart CMS, click "Online Devices," then click "Start Search" to scan for camera devices on the local network, as shown below.

In the search results, select the camera device whose settings you want to change, then click "Add to Management" to make it appear in the "Managed Devices" list, as shown below.

Go into "Main Preview," select the corresponding camera device, and configure tracking settings, as shown below.

Click "Parameter Settings," go into the "Basic 2" settings screen. There, change "Default State on Power-On" to "No Tracking," then click "Save" to finish, as shown below.

# 4. Setting Preset Positions

**Symptom:** once the camera is installed, you need to set preset positions to save the camera's position, focus, and image parameters in advance.

**Solution:** try the method below to resolve this.

**CAM34 camera:**

Setting a preset position: press a number key, then press the "Set Preset" key.

Recalling a preset position: press the corresponding number key + "CALL" key.

Clearing a preset position: press the corresponding number key + "CLEAR" key.

**CAM37 camera:**

Setting a preset position: press a number key, then press the "SET" key.

**P1 camera:**

Setting a preset position: press and hold a number key until the camera's indicator light blinks once, and the setting is complete.

# 5. Power Indicator Light Doesn't Turn On After Powering Up

**Symptom:** after the camera is powered on, the power indicator light doesn't turn on.

**Solution:** if the camera shows no response after powering up, and the power indicator light doesn't turn on, there may be a hardware fault — please contact ClassIn hardware after-sales support for repair or replacement.

# 6. Resetting the Preset Position

**Symptom:** needing to adjust the camera's preset position.

**Solution:** try either of the following methods to resolve this.

Press the corresponding number key directly to move the camera back to its preset position.

First press the corresponding number key, then press the CALL key, to move the camera back to its preset position.

# 7. No Image Displayed (CAM34)

**Symptom:** the CAM34 camera's blue POWER light and green STANDBY light are both on at the same time, but no image is displayed.

**Solution:** when the CAM34 camera's blue POWER light and green STANDBY light are both on, it means the camera is still starting up. Please wait patiently for about 20 seconds until only the blue POWER light stays on, indicating the camera has finished starting up. Only then will the image display normally.

# 8. Glitchy/Garbled Image (CAM37)

**Symptom:** the CAM37 camera's image appears glitchy or garbled.

First, check what type of cable connects the camera to the computer — usually an HDMI or SDI cable.

If you're using an HDMI or SDI cable, try replacing it with a new one.

If the issue persists after replacing the cable, we recommend connecting using the camera's included Mini USB cable instead.

# 9. No Image Displayed (CAM37)

**Symptom:** no image displays after connecting the CAM37 camera.

**Solution:** try any of the following methods to resolve this.

**Check the cable:** make sure the cable between the CAM37 camera and the computer is correctly and securely connected. Confirm the cable isn't damaged or loose.

**Confirm the power connection:** make sure the CAM37 camera's power is correctly connected, and its power indicator light is on normally.

**Try restarting:** use the power button on the remote control to try restarting the camera.

# 10. No Image Displayed (CAMP1)

**Symptom:** the CAMP1 camera's green light and red light are both on at the same time, but no image is displayed.

**Solution:** when the CAMP1 camera's green light and red light are both on, it means the camera is still starting up. Please wait patiently for about 20 seconds until the red light turns off, indicating the camera has finished starting up. Only then will the image display normally.

# 11. Disabling Auto-Zoom

**Symptom:** needing to disable the camera's auto-zoom feature.

Find the camera's remote control.

Press the "Manual" button on the remote.

# 12. Adjusting Camera Brightness (CAM34)

**Symptom:** needing to adjust the camera's image brightness.

**Solution:** follow these steps to adjust the CAM34 camera's brightness.

Press the "Menu" button on the CAM34 camera's remote control.

The camera's settings screen will pop up on your computer screen.

In the settings screen, select and go into the "Exposure" section.

Within "Exposure," find and adjust the "Brightness Compensation" option, until you reach a satisfying brightness level.

# 13. Adjusting Camera Focus

**Symptom:** needing to adjust the camera's focus value.

Use the "-" or "+" key on the remote control to adjust the focus.

# 13. USB Camera Not Recognized

**Symptom:** after installing the camera, the large display can't recognize this hardware.

Unplug and re-plug the USB connector on the computer side.

Restart the camera — ideally by cutting power and restarting it.

Open your computer's Device Manager, uninstall "Unknown USB Device," then reload/reinstall it.

If none of the above resolves it, consider whether the USB cable itself is faulty.
