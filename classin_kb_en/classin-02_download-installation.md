## Downloading and Installing ClassIn

## I. Installing ClassIn on Windows

### 1. Before You Download

**(1) Check your computer's specs**

Before downloading ClassIn, check your computer's hardware specs, including the CPU model, RAM, and operating system. Steps:
1. Right-click "This PC."
2. Select Properties.
3. Under Device Specifications, check the CPU model, RAM, and operating system.

### 2. Downloading and Installing ClassIn

**2.1 Usage Notes**

ClassIn supports Windows 7, Windows 8, Windows 10, Windows 11, and later. We recommend using Google Chrome or Firefox to download the ClassIn installer, as both browsers handle file downloads reliably.

To avoid installation issues, we recommend closing any antivirus or security software on your computer (such as 360 Total Security, 360 Antivirus, or Tencent PC Manager) before downloading the ClassIn installer. Such software can sometimes mistakenly flag ClassIn's registry files as a threat and delete them, disrupting the installation.

Official ClassIn download site (Chinese): https://www.eeo.cn/cn/download
Official ClassIn download site (English): https://www.classin.com/download

**2.2 Notes**

ClassIn does not support installation on: Windows XP, Windows 8.1 RT, or Windows Vista.

**2.3 Steps**
1. Open your browser.
2. Enter the following in the address bar: www.eeo.cn
3. Go to the Download Center.
4. Click to download the Windows version.
5. Once the download finishes, run the installer.
6. Follow the on-screen prompts to install ClassIn.

## II. Installing ClassIn on Mac

**(1) Check your CPU model**

Before downloading ClassIn, check your computer's CPU model. Steps:
1. Open Launchpad.
2. Search for "Terminal" and open it.
3. In the Terminal window, next to `LeondeMacbook-Air:~ shouhouzhichi$`, type `sysctl machdep.cpu.brand_string`.
4. Press Enter.
5. View your CPU model.

![](https://cofile.eeo.cn/res-store%2F2910229de3e8dc38f748ea122fe95ca3f0016f263bc74ff8c2d4db656ee8251a_2594456?q-sign-algorithm=sha1&q-ak=AKIDJQGsEOK2TfbFdEZifMnxrWx85mpdHj6H&q-sign-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-key-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-header-list=host&q-url-param-list=&q-signature=5e1435815a88a33835581c2ad92b4a33ca67cc6c)

**(2) Check your CPU chip type**

To check your Mac's RAM, OS version, and CPU chip type:
1. Hover over the Apple icon in the top-left corner.
2. Click About This Mac.
3. In the About This Mac window, check the RAM, OS version, and CPU chip type next to Processor.

macOS 10.13 and later supports downloading ClassIn. Since Mac computers currently on the market use one of two CPU chip types — Intel or Apple silicon — please check your CPU chip type and download the matching ClassIn installer from www.eeo.cn.

Official ClassIn download site (Chinese): https://www.eeo.cn/cn/download
Official ClassIn download site (English): https://www.classin.com/download

**2.2 Steps**

**Downloading and installing ClassIn for Intel chips**
1. Enter the following in your browser's address bar: www.eeo.cn
2. Click to download the macOS (Intel chip) version.
3. Run the installer and wait for it to verify.
4. Drag the ClassIn icon on the left into the Applications folder on the right, and wait for the copy to finish.
5. Open Launchpad and search for ClassIn.
6. Open ClassIn.
7. Wait for the first-launch verification, then click Open in the prompt that appears.

## III. Installing ClassIn on iPhone/iPad

**(1) Check your device model**

Open Settings → General → About, and check the model name.

**(2) Compare against the requirements table**

Compare your phone or tablet's model against the recommended devices below to confirm it meets ClassIn's requirements for class.

| | Minimum requirements (students / teachers not recording) | Minimum requirements (teachers recording) | Recommended requirements (teachers / students) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OS | iOS 11 or higher | iOS 11 or higher | iOS 14 or higher |
| iPad/iPhone models | iPhone 6 or later; iPad 5 or later; iPad mini 4 or later; iPad Air 3 or later; iPad Pro or later | iPhone 8 or later; iPad 6 or later; iPad Pro or later | iPhone XS or later; iPad 8 or later; iPad Pro 2 or later; iPad mini 5 or later; iPad Air 3 or later |
| Network bandwidth | At least 2Mbps | At least 4Mbps | At least 6Mbps |

If you're using an iPhone or iPad, make sure your iOS version is at least iOS 11 before downloading ClassIn. Then download ClassIn from the App Store:
1. Open the App Store.
2. Search for "ClassIn."
3. Tap Get on the right.

## IV. Installing ClassIn on Android Phones/Tablets

**Huawei phones/tablets**: Open Settings → scroll to the bottom → About Phone → check the CPU model next to Processor and the RAM next to Memory.

**Xiaomi phones/tablets**: Open Settings → My Device → check the CPU model next to Processor and the RAM next to Memory.

**OPPO phones/tablets**: Open Settings → scroll to the bottom → About Phone → check the CPU model next to Processor and the RAM next to Memory.

**vivo phones/tablets**: Open Settings → scroll to the bottom → About Phone → check the CPU model next to Processor and the RAM next to Memory.

Compare your phone or tablet's specs against the recommended processors and other specs below to confirm it meets ClassIn's requirements for class.

| | Minimum requirements (students / teachers not recording) | Minimum requirements (teachers recording) | Recommended requirements (teachers / students) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OS | Android 7.0 or higher; HarmonyOS 1.0 or higher | Android 10.0 or higher; HarmonyOS 2.0 or higher | Android 10.0 or higher; HarmonyOS 2.0 or higher |
| Memory | 4GB or higher | 8GB or higher | 8GB or higher |
| Processor | Qualcomm Snapdragon 7-series 730G or higher; Snapdragon 8-series 835 or higher; Kirin 8-series 810 or higher; Kirin 9-series 970 or higher; MediaTek Dimensity 720 or higher; MediaTek Helio G90T or higher | Snapdragon 8-series 855 or higher; Kirin 9-series 980 or higher; MediaTek Dimensity 1000 or higher | Snapdragon 8-series 865 or higher; Kirin 9-series 9000 or higher; MediaTek Dimensity 1100 or higher |
| Network bandwidth | At least 2Mbps | At least 4Mbps | At least 6Mbps |

If you're using an Android or HarmonyOS device, make sure your Android device is running at least Android 7.0, or your HarmonyOS device is running at least HarmonyOS 2.0, before downloading ClassIn. Steps:
1. Open your device's built-in app store.
2. Search for "ClassIn" in the search bar.
3. Tap Install on the right.
