## Personal Information

## I. Personal Information

### Usage Notes
ClassIn is an online education platform that not only gives students and teachers a convenient learning and teaching environment, but also lets users customize their personal information based on their preferences and needs. In ClassIn's personal information settings, users can add or edit their ClassIn avatar, nickname, gender, birthday, region, signature, and other details.

### Notes
A non-genuine phone number cannot be edited.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Personal Settings.
3. Set your avatar, nickname, gender, birthday, age, region, and signature.

#### Tablet
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Tap your nickname.
3. Set your personal information.

#### Phone
1. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.
2. Tap your nickname.

## II. Privacy Settings

In account privacy settings, you can configure: whether your personal information is public, and whether others can find you by phone number. Adjusting these privacy permissions helps effectively prevent your personal information from being exposed.

1. At the bottom, set how your personal information is shared, and how others can add you.

Click App Settings.
Click Privacy Settings.
Set how your personal information is shared, and how others can add you.

Click Home.

## Account and Security

## I. Viewing Your Account

### Applies to
Everyone.

### When to use this
If you can't see the classroom or activity you should be attending in ClassIn, check whether you're logged into the correct account. Being logged into the wrong account will make classrooms or activities you're supposed to attend invisible.

### Usage Notes
If you have multiple accounts, you can go to Account and Security to check which account is currently logged in, making it easier to manage and use them.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Account and Security.
3. View the currently logged-in account.

#### Tablet
1. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to Account and Security.

#### Phone
1. Go to Home.
2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner.

## II. Binding or Changing Your Phone Number

If a user registers for ClassIn using email, they'll need to bind a phone number after registration, so they can log in with it going forward.

If a user's phone number is no longer in use, they can change their current phone number to a new one in ClassIn — this way, all of their existing class data is preserved and won't be lost when they switch numbers. The new phone number must not already be registered with ClassIn, and you'll need to verify your current ClassIn login password to make the change. After the change, all class data under the old phone number will be transferred to the new number — including class records already completed, class playbacks, and so on — and you can then log in with the new phone number to continue using ClassIn.

### Notes
If a user's ClassIn account was registered with a phone number, or already has a phone number bound, they cannot bind again — to switch to a different number, use "change phone number" instead.

If a user is a primary account in the "Backend Management System," they cannot make this change themselves — please contact ClassIn support for help, at 400-077-1585.

A ClassIn account cannot be changed to a non-genuine phone number.

1. Bind or change your phone number.

If a user registered for ClassIn with a phone number, they can bind an email address, making it easier to log in going forward.

If a user's email is no longer in use, they can change their current email address in ClassIn. The new email must not already be registered with ClassIn, and you'll need to verify your current ClassIn login password to make the change. After the change, all class data under the old email will be transferred to the new email — including class records already completed, class playbacks, and so on — and you can then log in with the new email to continue using ClassIn.

1. Bind or change your email.

## V. Deactivating Your Account

If a user no longer wants to use ClassIn, they can log into ClassIn on a phone or tablet and deactivate their account. Once an account is successfully deactivated, every course and other content under it will be deleted together and cannot be recovered; the deactivated account will become unregistered. Please proceed carefully.

If a user's account is the primary account in the "Backend Management System," it cannot be deactivated.

If your account isn't tied to a genuine phone number, it cannot be deactivated.

If a user's account has unfinished class sessions or temporary classrooms under it, the account cannot be deactivated.

If a user's account is currently suspended, it cannot be deactivated.

1. Click Deactivate Account.

## VI. Device Management

If a user is logged into the same account on multiple devices, you can force-logout certain devices — from any device you're logged into, you can log another device out remotely. A device that's been force-logged-out will have its account and password information related to that account cleared.

1. Find the device you want to log out, then click its name to log it out.

Find the device you want to log out, then click Log Out.
