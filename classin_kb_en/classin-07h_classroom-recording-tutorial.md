## 2. Setting the Recording Area

### 2.1 Recording Only the Classroom View

Usage Notes: If you checked "Record ClassIn Classroom" when creating the class session, or you need to do a local recording, and the teacher on computer only wants to record what's inside the classroom, they can select this under "Recording Area" in ClassIn's classroom settings.

Choose "Minimum-Size Classroom Window" (once recording starts, the classroom window shrinks to 1280 x 720 and cannot be resized), or "Current-Size Classroom Window" (once recording starts, the classroom window can be resized by dragging, and cannot be minimized but can be maximized).

Notes:

If you choose to record the minimum-size or current-size classroom window, and the teacher then uses the screen share tool, or clicks the minimize button in the bottom-left corner while in Big Screen Mode, the entire computer desktop will be recorded instead.

This setting only applies to the current computer. When taking class on phone or tablet, the classroom window is full-screen by default, so the recording captures the phone or tablet's entire screen.

### 2.2 Recording the Entire Computer Desktop

Usage Notes: If you checked "Record ClassIn Classroom" when creating the class session, or you need to do a local recording, and the teacher on computer wants to record their entire computer screen, they can check "Entire Computer Desktop" under "Recording Area" in ClassIn's classroom settings. Once recording starts, everything inside and outside the classroom will be recorded; note this demands more from your computer's performance when using a larger display.

Notes: this setting only applies to the current computer. When taking class on phone or tablet, the classroom window is full-screen by default, so the recording captures the phone or tablet's entire screen.

## 3. Setting Recording Resolution

Usage Notes: If you checked "Record ClassIn Classroom" when creating the class session, or you need to do a local recording, and the teacher on computer wants to increase the recording's resolution, they can select it under "Recording Video Resolution" in ClassIn's classroom settings.

In HD mode, the recorded class video resolution is 1280 x 720; in Full HD mode, it's 1920 x 1080.

Notes: if the teacher's "Recording Area" is set to the minimum-size classroom window, Full HD resolution cannot be selected under "Recording Video Resolution." This setting only applies to the current computer.

## 4. Setting Recording Audio

wants to record all sound from the entire computer, they can select "System Sound" under "Recording Mode" in ClassIn's classroom settings. Once "System Sound" is checked, the recording captures all sounds on the computer, both inside and outside the classroom — for example, a video playing or music, or notification sounds from messaging software outside the classroom, will all be captured.

only wants to record sound from within the classroom, they can select "Software Sound" instead. Once "Software Sound" is checked, only sound from within the classroom is recorded, and sound from outside the classroom is not captured.

Notes: this setting only applies to the current computer.

## 5. Common Recording Issues

### 5.1 Preventing the Classroom Window from Shrinking After Recording

Once a teacher on computer checks "Current-Size Classroom Window" under "Recording Area" in ClassIn's classroom settings, the classroom window will no longer automatically shrink the next time they record.

### 5.2 Avoiding Recording What's Outside the Classroom

Once a teacher on computer checks "Minimum-Size Classroom Window" or "Current-Size Classroom Window" under "Recording Area" in ClassIn's classroom settings, the next recording will only capture what's inside the classroom, not what's outside it.

In addition, teachers on Windows 7 also need to set the system theme to the Aero theme.

### 5.3 Avoiding Recording Sound from Outside the Classroom

Once a teacher on computer checks "Software Sound" under "Recording Mode" in ClassIn's classroom settings, the next recording will only capture sound from within the classroom, not sound from outside it.

### 5.4 Preventing Recording Interruptions

While recording is in progress, don't plug or unplug speaker devices like headphones or external speakers — make sure the classroom's speaker output stays audible normally.

Don't voluntarily exit the classroom while recording is in progress.

Don't switch classroom mode (Standard Mode or Big Screen Mode) while recording is in progress.

Make sure the classroom microphone works normally while recording is in progress.

Make sure your network connection is stable while recording is in progress.

When recording on-site, make sure the selected camera feed in the on-site recording window is displaying properly.

## 6. Recording Status

### 6.1 Lead Teacher Checking "Record ClassIn Classroom" Status

Once a class session has "Record ClassIn Classroom" checked, once the lead teacher enters the classroom, they can tell whether recording is working properly based on the different recording-status indicators in the classroom.

Once recording starts, on computer (phone/tablet), a "TV" icon appears in the top-right (top-left) corner of the classroom window, from the lead teacher's view. When this icon is "green," it means the classroom recording is working normally; when "red," it means recording has been interrupted. (If Big Screen Mode is selected, check the recording status in the bottom-left corner of the classroom window instead.)

### 6.2 Lead Teacher Checking "Record On-Site" Status

Once a class session has "Record On-Site" checked, once the lead teacher enters the classroom, they can tell whether recording is working properly based on the different recording-status indicators in the classroom.

The Record On-Site feature is only supported for lead teachers on computer, so once recording starts, a "TV" icon containing the number "2" appears in the top-right corner of the classroom window, from the lead teacher's view. When this icon is "green," on-site recording is working normally; when "red," it has been interrupted. (If Big Screen Mode is selected, check the recording status in the bottom-left corner of the classroom window instead.)

### 6.3 Checking Local Recording Status

Once local recording starts, on computer (phone/tablet), a "camera" icon appears in the top-right (top-left) corner of the classroom window, from the lead teacher's view. When this icon is "green," it means the classroom recording is working normally; when "red," it means recording has been interrupted. (If Big Screen Mode is selected, check the recording status in the bottom-left corner of the classroom window instead.)

## II. The Difference Between Recording the Classroom, Recording On-Site, and OMO Livestream Broadcast

Cloud recording comes in two modes: "Record ClassIn Classroom" and "Record On-Site." These two recording modes can be enabled individually or together.

**Record ClassIn Classroom:** classroom recording mode has two view options — the first records only what's in the lead teacher's classroom window; the second records the lead teacher's entire computer desktop, including the classroom window and anything the user does on the desktop. Both view options can be configured under Recording Area in App Settings. If the lead teacher only wants to record what's inside the classroom window, they can check "Current-Size Classroom Window" under Recording Area. If the lead teacher wants to record both the ClassIn classroom view and everything happening on the computer desktop, they can check "Entire Computer Desktop" under Recording Area. When taking class on phone or tablet, the classroom window is full-screen by default, so the recording captures the phone or tablet's entire screen.

**Record On-Site:** this mode means that while the lead teacher is recording class, only the camera feed they've selected for on-site recording is captured — anything outside that camera view is not recorded. Online students in the classroom can open the on-site recording view window to watch the teacher's lesson enlarged and more clearly. Users watching class via the web livestream link can also directly switch to view the on-site recording feed. In addition, students watching the class playback afterward can likewise switch to on-site recording mode, for a better review and understanding of the teacher's explanation.

**OMO Livestream Broadcast:** if students only need to watch the teacher's livestream broadcast feed within the classroom, without needing cloud recording, we recommend using the OMO livestream broadcast feature. OMO livestream broadcast mode is a high-definition mode for recording the on-site scene — in this mode, only the camera feed the lead teacher selected in the OMO livestream broadcast settings is recorded in the classroom; anything outside that camera view is not recorded. Online students in the classroom can open the OMO livestream broadcast window to watch the teacher's lesson more clearly. This mode is especially suited to teaching scenarios that call for high-definition video.

When on-site recording mode and OMO livestream broadcast mode are both enabled, they cannot use different cameras separately — when the lead teacher selects a camera in on-site recording mode, OMO livestream broadcast automatically switches to that same camera, and vice versa.

On-site recording and OMO livestream broadcast are only supported for lead teachers using a computer or big screen (Windows) — other devices (such as phone/tablet) don't support them.

## III. ClassIn Classroom Recording

## What is "Cloud Recording"?
"Cloud Recording" includes two recording modes: "Record ClassIn Classroom" and "Record On-Site." Once recording is checked when creating the class session, ClassIn's built-in recording feature fully records the entire class from the lead teacher's point of view while they teach. These recorded videos are automatically uploaded and stored on ClassIn's servers. About half an hour after class ends, the recorded video is turned into a "class playback video" for students and teachers to review and watch afterward.

## Applies to
Lead teacher.

## Prerequisites
"Record ClassIn Classroom" under "Cloud Recording" must be checked when creating the class session before the lead teacher can use the "cloud recording classroom recording" feature during class.

## Usage Notes
Recording ClassIn Classroom means recording what's happening inside the classroom itself, from the lead teacher's view.

If the lead teacher on computer selects the current-size or minimized classroom window as the recording area, the classroom view is recorded; but if the lead teacher uses the screen share tool, or minimizes the window from the bottom-left corner in Big Screen Mode, the entire computer desktop where the ClassIn classroom window is located gets recorded instead.

If the lead teacher on computer selects the entire computer desktop as the recording area, the entire computer desktop where the ClassIn classroom window is located is recorded.

Every time the lead teacher enters the classroom, they receive a recording prompt; after clicking Confirm, a "recording countdown" prompt appears. The lead teacher can manually click "Start Recording Now" on the countdown prompt to begin right away, or wait for the countdown to finish and let recording start automatically. The default recording countdown is 10 minutes; to change this, please have a teacher with "Backend Management" access change the countdown time in the "Backend Management System."

Recording cannot be stopped once class has started; if you don't want to record, you can only turn off "Record ClassIn Classroom" the next time you create a class session. Recording stops automatically once the lead teacher exits the classroom, and starts prompting again once they re-enter.

If the lead teacher forgot to enable "Record ClassIn Classroom" before class, they can use the "Local Recording" feature to record and save the teaching session locally on their computer or big screen, or create a new class session and check "Record ClassIn Classroom" this time.

## Notes
An unstable network connection for the lead teacher may cause recording to fail.

If recording fails, ClassIn will automatically retry; after 3 consecutive failed attempts, a prompt appears in the classroom offering to switch from cloud recording to local recording, which the lead teacher can accept manually (only computer supports this switch — phone and tablet cannot). Once switched to local recording, the recorded file is by default stored in a new folder under ClassIn file, in mp4 format by default; local recording can be manually stopped and restarted. If you don't click to switch to local recording, cloud recording continues and ClassIn keeps retrying; the switch prompt won't reappear, but you can reopen it by clicking the red "recording failed" icon.

Also, if the lead teacher exits and re-enters the classroom, ClassIn will retry cloud recording again.

If the lead teacher plugs or unplugs a speaker device on their computer, or the speaker disconnects due to an unstable connection, this can also stop the recording. In that case, the lead teacher will see a "Recording Interrupted" prompt; to keep recording, reconnect the speaker device or switch to a more stable one, then click Retry in the classroom's "Recording Interrupted" prompt window.

While recording, if the lead teacher uses the "Screen Share" tool in the classroom, or clicks "Minimize" in the bottom-left corner in Big Screen Mode, everything on the computer desktop where the ClassIn classroom is located will be recorded.

On Android devices, close features like split-screen, floating windows, or landscape mode before recording — if these features are left on, recording may fail.

2. Read the recording prompt, then click Confirm.

4. Check the recording status.

## IV. ClassIn On-Site Recording

"Record On-Site" under "Cloud Recording" must be checked when creating the class session before the lead teacher can use the "cloud recording on-site recording" feature during class.

On-site recording means that while a lead teacher on computer is recording class, only the camera feed they've selected in the on-site recording window is captured — anything outside that camera view is not recorded, so make sure the selected camera feed is displaying properly.

Every time the lead teacher enters the classroom, they receive a recording prompt; after clicking Confirm, they'll see the on-site recording window along with a "recording countdown" prompt.

In this window, you can view the current on-site recording feed, and switch which camera device to record using settings in the top-right corner of the window; recording only works properly if the on-site recording feed is displaying normally.

Once the lead teacher has adjusted the on-site recording camera feed, they can manually click "Start Recording Now" on the countdown prompt to begin right away, or wait for the countdown to finish and let recording start automatically. The default recording countdown is 10 minutes; to change this, please have a teacher with "Backend Management" access change the countdown time in the "Backend Management System."

Recording cannot be stopped once class has started; if you don't want to record, you can disable the camera in the on-site recording window, or turn off "Record ClassIn Classroom" the next time you create a class session. Recording stops automatically once the lead teacher exits the classroom, and starts prompting again once they re-enter.

In addition, online students in the classroom can also view the "Live Broadcast" feed (i.e. the on-site recording feed) in the bottom-right corner.

On-site recording is only supported for lead teachers on computer.

An unstable network connection for the lead teacher may cause recording to fail. The recording icon will turn red, and ClassIn will automatically retry recording.

## V. Local Recording

### What is "Local Recording"?
"Local Recording" refers to a teacher using ClassIn's screen recording feature (recording the classroom window) to record the teaching session and save it locally to a computer, or to the system photo album on a phone or tablet.

### Prerequisites
As long as either "Record ClassIn Classroom" or "Record On-Site" is enabled (or both), the class session's co-teacher can use local recording.

If neither "Record ClassIn Classroom" nor "Record On-Site" is enabled, the class session's co-teacher can still use local recording.

An academic teacher who enters classroom monitoring via the "Monitor Classes" module in the "Backend Management System" can use local recording.

Anyone who enters the "Whiteboard" from the ClassIn main interface can use "Local Recording."

Local recording can be used after creating and entering a temporary classroom in a class or friend chat.

A teacher who enters the preparation room can use "Local Recording."

### When to use this
If a class session has a co-teacher, and the lead teacher's cloud recording of the classroom becomes unstable (for example: recording is interrupted, and the recording-status icon in the classroom turns "red"), the co-teacher can use local recording in the classroom to make sure the lesson gets fully recorded without gaps. Afterward, the academic teacher can upload the co-teacher's local recording video to the "Backend Management System" to replace the lead teacher's incomplete recording of that class session.

If a class session has no co-teacher, and the lead teacher's cloud recording becomes unstable (for example: recording is interrupted, and the recording-status icon in the classroom turns "red"), the academic teacher can log into the "Backend Management System," go to classroom monitoring under "Monitor Classes," and do a local recording. Afterward, the academic teacher can upload their own local recording video to the "Backend Management System" to replace the lead teacher's incomplete recording of that class session.

For a classroom where neither "Record ClassIn Classroom" nor "Record On-Site" is enabled, but you need to record the classroom content during class, the lead teacher or co-teacher can use local recording, then send the local playback video afterward to whoever needs to watch it.

### Usage Notes
Once the prerequisites for local recording are met, the lead teacher clicks the recording icon, selects a save location and file type (flv/mp4, default flv) in the save-path window, and recording begins. Local recording captures what's happening inside the classroom.

If the teacher on computer selects the current-size or minimized classroom window as the recording area, the classroom view is recorded; but if the teacher uses the screen share tool, or minimizes the window from the bottom-left corner in Big Screen Mode, the entire computer desktop where the ClassIn classroom window is located gets recorded instead.

If the teacher on computer selects the entire computer desktop as the recording area, the entire computer desktop where the ClassIn classroom window is located is recorded.

Clicking the recording icon again ends local recording. You can start local recording again afterward.

### Notes
Requirements for local recording on phone or tablet: iOS 11 or higher; for Android, the processor must be Qualcomm Snapdragon 700 series or higher, or an equivalent-performance processor. In addition, the recorded video file grows by about 12MB per minute, so make sure your phone or tablet has enough storage space. We also recommend keeping classroom sound enabled and working properly.

An unstable teacher network connection may cause recording to fail. You can manually try restarting the recording if it fails.

If the teacher plugs or unplugs a speaker device on their computer, or the speaker disconnects due to an unstable connection, this can also stop the recording. In that case, the lead teacher will see a "Recording Interrupted" prompt; to keep recording, reconnect the speaker device or switch to a more stable one, then click Retry in the classroom's "Recording Interrupted" prompt window.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. In Standard Mode, click the recording icon in the top-right corner of the classroom (in Big Screen Mode, click Record under More in the bottom-left corner).
2. Read the local recording prompt, then click Start Recording.
3. Select a save path and file type, then click Save.
4. Check the recording status. Click the recording icon again to End the recording.

#### Android Phone or Tablet
1. Click the recording icon in the top-left corner of the classroom.
2. Click the recording icon in the top-left corner again to stop recording; recording also stops if you exit the classroom.
3. Go to your local photo album to watch the local recording's playback video.

#### iOS Phone or iPad
1. Read the local recording prompt, then click Confirm.
2. Click Screen Recording.
3. Check the recording status (the icon in the top-left corner turns green when recording).
4. Go to your local Photos app to watch the local recording's playback video.

## VI. Switching to Local Recording When ClassIn Classroom Recording Fails

When an unstable network connection for the lead teacher causes classroom recording to fail, ClassIn will automatically retry; after 3 consecutive failed attempts, a prompt appears in the classroom offering to switch from cloud recording to local recording, which the lead teacher can accept manually (only computer supports this switch — phone and tablet cannot). Once switched to local recording, the recorded file is by default stored in a new folder under ClassIn file, in mp4 format by default; local recording can be manually stopped and restarted. If you don't click to switch to local recording, cloud recording continues and ClassIn keeps retrying; the switch prompt won't reappear, but you can reopen it by clicking the red "recording failed" icon.

After class ends, when the teacher checks the playback under "Schedule" or "Class" in ClassIn, a small red dot appears on the playback button, and clicking it shows: "You have a local recording file pending upload — please contact an administrator to upload the recording video." Clicking "Local Recording File" opens the file's storage location. You can send the locally recorded video to an academic teacher, who can log into the Backend Management System and upload it to the corresponding class session's playback slot. If there's no local recording file (for example, it was deleted), no such prompt appears.

1. Read the recording error prompt, then click Switch Now.
