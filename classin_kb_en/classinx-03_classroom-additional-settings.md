## ClassInX Classroom Additional Settings

# I. Showing/Hiding the On-Stage Seats Area

## Usage Notes
The on-stage seats area is the row at the very top of the whiteboard. It's used only to display teacher and student camera feeds — you cannot write on it.

Besides choosing whether to show the on-stage seats area when creating a class session, once inside the classroom, the teaching teacher or co-teacher can also show or hide the on-stage seats area from the classroom's additional settings.

Also, the teacher's on-stage seat rotation setting only takes effect while the on-stage seats area is shown. When the teacher needs to write full-screen, they can hide the on-stage seats area.

## Steps
1. Tap or swipe up at the bottom-center of the screen to open the dock.
2. Click Settings on the far right.
3. Click Additional Settings.
4. Click General.
5. Choose to Show or Hide the on-stage seats area.

# II. Focus Mode

## Applies to
Teaching teacher, co-teacher.

To keep students from getting distracted during class, we recommend using Focus Mode. Backend administrators can apply a skin with "Enable Focus Mode" checked to a class, and formal classrooms under that class will have Focus Mode enabled by default. In addition, once the teaching teacher or co-teacher enters the classroom, they can also turn Focus Mode on or off from the classroom's additional settings.

Once Focus Mode is enabled: if a student enters the classroom on a Windows computer, the classroom window will stay maximized and pinned on top, unobstructed by other windows. If a student enters the classroom on a phone or tablet, switching to another app will automatically exit the classroom.

Focus Mode set by the teaching teacher or co-teacher within the classroom only applies to the current classroom.

## Notes
Focus Mode does not take effect for students using a Mac OS device.

Focus Mode does not take effect for auditors.

If a student's device supports split-screen or similar features, Focus Mode may also fail to take effect.

1. Turn Focus Mode on or off.

# III. Raise Hand Sound Alert

## When to use this
When a student raises their hand in the classroom, the teacher's device automatically plays a raise-hand alert sound, so the teacher can still notice a student's raised hand while focused on teaching, writing on the board, or viewing files, and won't miss a chance to interact.

Once inside the classroom, the teaching teacher and co-teacher can each set their own raise-hand alert sound independently, with no effect on each other.

In raise-hand alert settings, teachers can choose no alert sound, or select a "ding," "tick-tock," or "ding-dong" sound as the alert.

The alert sound is only audible to the teacher themself — students in the classroom won't hear it.

1. Select a Raise-Hand Alert Sound.

# IV. Entry/Exit Notifications

Once inside the classroom, if the teacher enables entry/exit notifications in the classroom's additional settings, a pop-up notification will appear at the bottom of the teacher's screen whenever a student joins or leaves the classroom, helping the teacher track attendance status in real time.

The teaching teacher and co-teacher can each set their own entry/exit notifications independently, with no effect on each other.

If the teaching teacher has this feature enabled, they'll also be notified when a co-teacher joins or leaves.

1. Turn entry/exit notifications on or off.

# V. Classroom Background

A classroom background can be set in advance in the Backend Management System; once inside the classroom, teachers can also set it themselves under the classroom's additional settings. Once a teacher changes the classroom background, the new one takes effect immediately.

Teachers can choose from the system's built-in background images, or upload a local image as the classroom background. Uploaded images must be JPG format and no larger than 1MB.

A classroom background changed within the classroom's additional settings only applies to the current classroom.

1. Click Classroom Background.
2. Select or upload a classroom background image.

# VI. Classroom Window Layout

Dragging cameras, courseware, and widgets to preset positions with automatic resizing helps teachers quickly organize the classroom whiteboard interface, efficiently switch teaching scenarios, and stay focused on instruction and interaction.

Once inside the classroom, teachers can go to Classroom Additional Settings → Other → Window Layout to choose an existing layout template. Custom layout templates are also supported — you can add a template using either tiled mode (pane size and position are fixed and cannot be freely adjusted) or free-pane mode (pane size and position can be freely adjusted).

Once you've selected a template, if the teacher is using a large display, they can drag a camera, courseware, or widget with one finger and hold for 0.5 seconds to enter layout mode; if using a computer, they can drag with the mouse while holding Shift on the keyboard to enter layout mode.

1. Click Other.
2. Under Layout Settings, click Window Layout.
3. Select or add a layout template.
4. Drag a camera/courseware/widget with one finger, holding for 0.5 seconds to enter layout mode.
(On computer, hold Shift while dragging.)

# VII. Classroom Help Request

Teaching teacher, co-teacher, class session students (excluding auditors).

When a teacher or student in the classroom runs into an issue they can't resolve on their own, they can request assistance from the backend academic administrator.

Once inside the classroom, teachers can go to Classroom Additional Settings → Other → Help Request to open the help request window. Select the person(s) with the issue, describe the issue, then click Confirm. The backend academic administrator can view the classroom help request information under Academic Management → Classroom Monitoring at www.eeo.cn, including the issue description and a system-captured screenshot from every user's classroom view at that moment. Once the academic teacher replies, a reply window will pop up automatically on the teacher's screen in the classroom.

Once backend administrators apply a skin with "Allow Students to Send Help Requests to the School Backend" enabled to a class, students using a computer or iPad to enter a classroom under that class can then use the help request feature. In the help request window, students describe the issue and click Confirm. The backend academic administrator can view the classroom help request information under Academic Management → Classroom Monitoring at www.eeo.cn, including the issue description and a system-captured screenshot from that student's classroom view. Once the academic teacher replies, a reply window will pop up automatically on that student's screen in the classroom.

Students (except auditors) can only use the help request feature if they enter, via the ClassIn app on computer or iPad, a classroom under a class with "Allow Students to Send Help Requests to the School Backend" enabled.

1. Under Quick Help Request, click Help Request.
2. Teacher: select the person(s) with the issue, describe the issue, then click Confirm.
(Student: describe the issue, then click Confirm.)

# VIII. Upgrading ClassInX Software

Everyone.

To ensure smoother use, we recommend upgrading ClassInX software to the latest version.

Users who aren't logged in can check the current ClassInX software version and upgrade it via the dock at the very bottom of the screen → Settings → Additional Settings → About.

Logged-in users can also check the current ClassInX software version and upgrade it via the dock at the very bottom of the screen → ClassIn → avatar in the bottom-left corner → About.

1. Click About.
2. Check the current ClassInX software version, or click Upgrade.

# IX. Classroom Stylus Settings

When using a stylus to write on the whiteboard in ClassInX, if the stylus is meant to be used only for writing, we recommend enabling "Stylus for Writing Only" via the dock at the very bottom of the screen → Settings → Additional Settings → Hardware.

Once enabled, the stylus is used only for writing, and finger touches will behave according to whichever tool is currently selected on the radial dial (note: tilting the stylus tip may cause it to be misidentified as a finger touch).

1. Click Hardware.
2. Turn "Stylus for Writing Only" on or off.

# X. Classroom Touch Lock Settings

To prevent students in an offline classroom from accidentally touching the large display's screen, teachers can enable "Lock Screen Touch" during breaks via the ClassInX software's dock at the very bottom of the screen → Settings → Additional Settings → Hardware.

When touch lock is on, users can turn it off by tapping the touch lock button at the bottom of the screen, or by pressing and holding the "Previous Page" button on the page-turner remote.

When touch lock is off, users can turn it on the same way — by pressing and holding "Previous Page" on the page-turner remote.

1. Turn "Lock Screen Touch" on or off.

# XI. DaKe Voice Assistant

The DaKe voice assistant helps users complete ClassIn-related operations on a ClassInX 2.0 large display more conveniently through voice commands.

The voice assistant feature is only supported on ClassInX 2.0 large display devices, and requires microphone permission to be enabled in the ClassInX software.

In the ClassInX software's dock at the very bottom of the screen → Settings → Voice Assistant, you can turn the "DaKe Voice Assistant" feature on or off.

Once the DaKe voice assistant is enabled, you can also turn on or off: "Wake-Free Mode" (no need to say "DaKe DaKe" to wake it — you can speak commands directly), "Text Feedback" (voice recognition results display as text on the large display), and "Sound Feedback" (DaKe responds with a "ding" sound). These features can also be turned on or off by tapping the DaKe icon in the bottom-right corner of the classroom or whiteboard.

In addition, in the formal classroom, the teaching teacher, co-teacher, and group leaders can use the DaKe voice assistant feature when eligible; eligible students do not have this feature's permission.

1. Click Voice Assistant.
2. Turn the DaKe Voice Assistant on or off.
