## Classroom Courseware

## I. Opening Courseware

### Applies to
Lead teacher, co-teacher(s), authorized students.

### Usage Notes
In the ClassIn classroom, you can open courseware already uploaded to your cloud drive — for example, opening a Word document, playing an audio file (music, MP3), or playing a video file (MP4) all count as opening uploaded courseware.

### Notes
The classroom only shows organization cloud drive files belonging to the current class session's school.

### Steps

#### Computer
**Personal courseware:** open the cloud drive, go to My Cloud Drive, and open locally uploaded courseware.

**School/institution-authorized courseware:** open the cloud drive, go to Organization Cloud Drive, go to School Materials, and open the authorized courseware.

**Organization courseware:** open the cloud drive, go to Organization Cloud Drive, and open organization courseware.

**Resource Center courseware:** open the cloud drive, go to Resource Center, go to My Resources, and open courseware obtained from the Resource Center.

## III. PPT Animations and Video Playback

## Applies to
## Usage Notes
In the classroom, if you need to play a PPT's animation effects or an embedded video, use the "Click Tool." Specifically: for animation effects, click the location in the PPT where the animation is, or click anywhere on the PPT slide to trigger it. For an embedded video, you need to click the play button inside the video frame to play it. If you don't click the play button inside the video frame, ClassIn won't trigger playback, and may jump straight past the current page to the next one.

## V. PDF Page Navigation, Thumbnail Browsing, and Synchronized Scrolling

## When to use this
The following scenarios call for the PDF thumbnail feature:

In the classroom, when a user opens a PDF courseware document with many pages, they can use the PDF thumbnail feature to more easily see the document's outline. By showing PDF thumbnails, users can quickly get a sense of the whole document's content structure and jump directly to a specific page. This makes it easier to navigate and locate the information you need, improving the experience of using PDF courseware.

When a user is showing a PDF courseware document in the classroom that wasn't fully covered last time, they can use the PDF thumbnail feature to quickly jump back to where they left off. By clicking the relevant thumbnail, users can quickly jump to the page they need and precisely resume content that wasn't fully shown before. This significantly reduces the time spent searching for a particular page, improving classroom presentation efficiency.

On phone and tablet, once a PDF is opened, page navigation is not supported.

Phone and tablet cannot view PDF thumbnails.

### (1) PDF page navigation
1. Click the number below the courseware to jump to a page.

## VI. Adjusting Courseware Volume

Everyone.

In the classroom, if a video courseware's volume sounds too quiet, you can adjust the volume for that audio/video courseware in the classroom, no matter who opened it — every classroom member can adjust its volume independently, since the adjustment only affects what you personally hear and doesn't affect anyone else.

Adjustments a user makes to audio/video courseware volume only take effect for that user, and won't affect what other classroom members hear.

On phone and tablet, the only way to make video sound louder is by adjusting the device's own volume.

### (1) Adjusting video courseware volume
1. Click the speaker icon in the bottom-right corner of the video courseware to adjust the volume.

## IX. Adjusting Courseware Display Size

Lead teacher, co-teacher(s), authorized students.

### When to use this
After a user opens a piece of courseware in the classroom, if the courseware window feels too small or too large, they can resize it. Resizing the window lets users browse the courseware more comfortably and enjoy a better visual experience. Resizable courseware includes: PDF courseware, PPT courseware, audio, video, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and any other file type the classroom supports opening.

#### Computer:
**Drag to resize:** hover the mouse over the edge of the courseware window; once the cursor turns into a double-headed arrow, click and drag the border to resize the window.

**Maximize window:** if the courseware window isn't already maximized, click the "Maximize" button in the top-right corner of the window to maximize it.

**Restore window size:** if the courseware window is already maximized, click the "Restore" button in the top-right corner of the window to return it to its non-maximized size.

#### Phone/tablet:
**Two-finger pinch:** place two fingers on the courseware area of the screen and pinch in or spread out to shrink or enlarge the courseware window.
