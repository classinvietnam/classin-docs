## Lesson Pack (Multi-Courseware Bundle)

## I. What Is a Lesson Pack?

A lesson pack is a tool that bundles multiple teaching resources together, helping teachers find and use the resources they need more conveniently. By merging several pieces of courseware into a single lesson pack, teachers can organize and curate courseware while preparing lessons, making it easier to use in class.

## II. Creating a New Lesson Pack

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
Teachers on computer can create a lesson pack in My Cloud Drive and select the files they need. During a formal class, they can then use it by opening Classroom → My Cloud Drive → Lesson Pack.

### Notes
Phone and tablet cannot create a "Lesson Pack."

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Go to Space.
2. Click New.
3. Select Lesson Pack.

## EDX Authoring Tutorial

### I. What Is an EDX File?
An EDX file is a special file that contains a website address. When a teacher opens an EDX file during class, it launches the Multi-Directional Browser to open the URL inside the file; the way students and the teacher interact with it is the same as when the teacher opens the Multi-Directional Browser tool directly.

### II. Creating an EDX File

**Step 1:**

Get the URL of the website you'll need during class — for example, www.eeo.cn.

**Step 2:**

Create a new EDX file, and fill in the file's name and the website address.

Note: once created, you can open it directly for a preview to check whether the webpage displays correctly.

**Step 3:**

During a formal class, the teacher can open this EDX file from the cloud drive; once opened, class can proceed exactly as with the Multi-Directional Browser.

Note: within the classroom, this file type can only be opened by the teacher — no other role has permission to open it. Outside the classroom, there's no such restriction, and it can be previewed normally.

## CamIn

## I. What Is CamIn?

CamIn is an all-in-one, easy-to-use video creation tool. It combines a virtual camera, video production, and live streaming push features. It's designed to help every user easily create polished videos.

CamIn supports use on computer, and CamIn's cloud space can be viewed within the ClassIn software's Space.

### Related Articles
CamIn Help Center

CamIn Official Website

## II. Uploading a Video / Creating a New Folder

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
Users on computer or tablet can view and use Flowin's cloud space within Space, where they can upload videos and create new folders. Videos support sharing, renaming, editing the cover, moving, duplicating, downloading, and deleting; folders support sharing, renaming, moving, and deleting.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to Space.
2. Go to CamIn.
3. Click New → Upload Video / New Folder.
4. Select the video or folder action.

## III. Team Space

CamIn's cloud space supports creating and managing a team space; the team edition is a video production and management tool suited to multi-person collaboration. Users on computer or tablet can switch teams, invite and manage members, and upload videos or create new folders within the CamIn module of the ClassIn software.

1. Expand the side toolbar.
2. Switch or create a team.
3. Invite team members.
4. Go to Team Space.
5. Go to Space Management and Space Settings.

## Nobook

## I. What Is Nobook?

Nobook is a smart teaching tool built for teachers and students, designed for hands-on physics, chemistry, and biology experiments in middle and high school, as well as elementary science demonstrations. Teachers can develop courseware online as needed, and freely build and assemble experiments, achieving a cloud-based, intelligent, fully open experiment environment.

Users on computer or tablet can view and use Nobook's middle/high school physics, chemistry, and biology experiments within the ClassIn software's Space.

### Related Articles
NOBOOK Official Website

## II. Switching Subjects and Binding Membership

### Applies to
Everyone, or users with NOBOOK membership privileges.

### Usage Notes
Users on computer or tablet can view and use Nobook within Space. Nobook covers six subjects across middle and high school — physics, chemistry, and biology at each level — and users can switch subjects as needed. If you've purchased a membership on NOBOOK, you can bind your membership account to use VIP experiments; once bound, you can also check the expiration date of your subject-level VIP access.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to Space.
2. Go to Nobook.
3. Switch subject.
4. Bind your NOBOOK membership account.
5. Check your subject VIP expiration date.

## III. Experiments

Users on computer or tablet can find an experiment and view its demo video and description, and can also edit or DIY their own experiment. Within an experiment, you can select the move tool to reposition equipment, fill in an experiment report, equations/tables, and watch the demo video while working through it. Once you finish an experiment, you can save it; saved experiments can be viewed, edited, or deleted under My Experiments.

1. Search for an experiment.
2. View or edit the experiment.
3. Click Save after editing the experiment.
4. Go to My Experiments.

## TeacherIn

## I. What Is TeacherIn?

TeacherIn is a platform for the education field, mainly offering course publishing, sales, management, and related teaching services, suited to teachers, educational institutions, and independent operators.

TeacherIn integrates deeply with the ClassIn teaching platform — teachers can apply courses from TeacherIn directly to their classes in ClassIn, enabling a seamless connection between online and offline teaching.

### Related Articles
TeacherIn Help Center

TeacherIn Official Website

TI Store

## II. Creating a Course

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
Users on computer or tablet can view TeacherIn within Space. In TI, you can view published courses, and can also go to the workspace to build your own course.

### Steps

#### Computer/Tablet
1. Go to Space.
2. Go to TeacherIn.
3. Go to the Workspace.
4. Create a course.

## III. Applying/Sharing/Publishing/Deleting a Course

Space supports creating a course, and within a course you can create units and activities. In addition:

Teachers can apply activities from a course to any class where they're the head teacher or a teacher.

Courses support sharing with other users to co-edit.

Courses support publishing — once published, a course can be found and used on the TeacherIn platform.

The course page supports opening it in a browser, course settings (such as file download permissions), and deleting it.

1. Find and enter the course.
2. Create a new unit and activity.
3. Apply / Share / Publish / Delete the course.

## IV. Organization Space

Users can create an organization space in TI, where courses can be jointly managed and created, and also sold. Space also has a certain capacity limit — once exceeded, it can no longer be used. Users on computer or tablet can switch to or create an organization space in My Space, and can also check their personal space capacity.

1. Go to My Space.
2. Switch to / create an organization, or check personal space capacity.

## Flowin

## I. What Is Flowin?

Flowin is a content creation platform for the AI era, designed to help users create content, collaborate as a team, and manage knowledge in a variety of ways. It supports freely combining and dragging to lay out elements like text, images, audio, video, tables, and code blocks, letting users flexibly create rich, polished content. Flowin also offers team collaboration and permission control features, suited to joint creation and project management. In addition, Flowin integrates AI capabilities that can help users create content from scratch or refine existing content, improving creation efficiency and quality. Flowin is used across a wide range of scenarios, including teaching and research, work support, campus life, and content creator work. It also supports publishing and monetizing content, encouraging quality content creators to share knowledge through the platform and earn income.

Flowin offers multi-platform support, including web and mobile (iOS and Android), letting users create and collaborate anytime, anywhere.

Users on computer or tablet can view and use the Flowin web version within the ClassIn software's Space.

### Related Articles
Flowin Help Center

Flowin Official Website

## II. Creating a New Space / Document

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
Users on computer or tablet can view and use Flowin within Space. Once inside a personal or organization space in Flowin, you can view or create documents. Flowin documents support text, headings, tables, emoji, formulas, @mentioning members, AI, and other features.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Go to Space.
2. Go to Flowin.
3. Enter or create a space.
4. Switch between personal/organization space.
5. View or create a new document.

#### Tablet
1. Choose a personal or organization space in the workspace.
2. Go to the directory.

## III. Personal Storage

Flowin space has a certain capacity limit — once exceeded, it can no longer be used. On computer, users can find Flowin within the ClassIn software's Space and go there to check their personal storage usage.

1. Expand the side toolbar.
2. Click your avatar → Account Settings.
3. Check My Benefits.

## Resource Center

## I. What Is the Resource Center?

The Resource Center brings together a wealth of teaching material resources for teachers and students to use. Based on your specific needs, you can search for the textbook you need by choosing conditions such as grade, school level, subject, and publisher. In the Resource Center, you can find textbook materials across every subject — Chinese, math, English, history, geography, physics, and more. You can select the appropriate textbook based on the teaching requirements of different grades. In addition, the Resource Center offers textbooks across multiple school levels — elementary, middle, and high school — for students at different grades. You can select the corresponding school level to find the matching textbook resources. The Resource Center also offers textbooks from different publishers, since publishers' textbooks can vary somewhat, letting you choose the publisher's edition that suits your teaching needs. Through the Resource Center, teachers and students can conveniently access the teaching materials they need, helping improve teaching outcomes and learning results.

## II. Getting Courseware from the "Resource Center"

### Applies to
Everyone.

### Usage Notes
ClassIn offers a rich Resource Center covering online textbooks for every grade and subject across elementary, middle, and high school. These textbooks are available for teachers and students to support their teaching and learning. Besides textbooks, ClassIn also offers curated exam papers and featured topic collections. These exam papers help students practice and test their learning outcomes, while the featured topic collections offer an in-depth explanation and analysis of a specific topic, helping students better understand and master the material.

In addition, ClassIn offers courseware specific to the Beijing Dongcheng district and Shandong Qingyun district. This courseware is produced according to local education requirements and teaching realities, helping local teachers run their lessons more effectively. Users can search for courseware by publisher, or search for resources by keyword. This integration and provisioning makes it easier for teachers and students to access relevant educational resources, improving their teaching efficiency and learning outcomes.

### Steps

#### Computer
1. Go to Space in the left navigation.
2. Go to Resource Center.
3. Go to All Resources.
4. Find the courseware by category or search, then click Get once you've found it.

## III. Deleting Courseware Obtained from the "Resource Center"

Delete courseware from your personal cloud drive, or delete courseware obtained from the Resource Center.

### Notes
Phone and tablet cannot delete courseware obtained from the "Resource Center."

## Steps
1. Go to Space.
2. Go to My Resources.
3. Find the resource courseware you want to delete, then click Delete.
