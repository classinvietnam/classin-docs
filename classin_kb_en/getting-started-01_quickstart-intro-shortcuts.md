## Quick Start

## Step 1: Download and Install ClassIn

Go to the ClassIn official website (www.eeo.cn), then go to the Download Center.

Download the installer and follow the on-screen prompts to install ClassIn.

## Step 2: Log Into ClassIn

Enter your registered account to log into ClassIn.

## Step 3: Set Up Your Personal Information

Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the software.

Set your avatar and nickname.

## Step 4: Device Check

Before entering the classroom, "check" your network connection and audio/video devices to make sure you can take class normally. Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the software, go to Device Check, and follow the on-screen prompts to run the check.

## Step 5: Uploading Courseware

Before class, teachers can go to Space from the left navigation and upload local courseware to Space, making it ready to use during class. (If you don't have any courseware to show, you can skip this step.)

## Step 6: Entering the Classroom

Find the corresponding class session at the top of the software, then click "Start Class" to enter the classroom.

## Step 7: Checking Your Appearance

After clicking "Start Class," you'll enter the screen for adjusting your microphone and camera.

Check your appearance here, then click "Enter Classroom" once you're ready.

## Step 8: Common Classroom Tools

Once the teacher enters the classroom, they can control their camera and microphone in the top-right corner of the classroom, with common teaching tools on the right side. To show or hide student cameras, use the last tool in the right toolbar — "Roster."

Once a student enters the classroom, they can control their camera and microphone in the top-right corner of the classroom; by default, only three tools appear on the right — "Class Notes," "Chat Window," and "Roster."

## Step 9: Watching the Class Playback Video After Class

If cloud recording was enabled for the class session, about half an hour after class ends you can go to Schedule and find that session's playback video by date.

## ClassIn Introduction

# I. ClassIn Introduction

### 1. Product Concept and Supported Subjects
ClassIn pursues "professionalism" in teaching, dedicated to delivering an outstanding teaching experience for users.

It supports a wide range of subjects, including children's English, language training, professional training, STEAM, and can even be used to teach specialized subjects like horsemanship, dance, and seal engraving.

### 2. Teaching Scenarios
ClassIn can perfectly support many teaching scenarios — one-on-one teaching, small classes, large classes, livestreaming, dual-teacher (domestic and foreign teacher) instruction, and more — meeting the needs of different educational institutions and users.

### 3. Mobile Teaching and Interactivity
ClassIn offers strong mobility — teachers can livestream a lecture from real-world settings like Yellowstone National Park, a planetarium, or an art exhibition, and students can also share and present what they've seen and experienced during an online class.

It supports synchronized face-to-face teaching for up to 16 people, with both teachers and students able to use video windows and audio to interact. Video windows can be freely dragged and resized, suited to small-group learning, discussion, and presentation scenarios.

### 4. Personalized Teaching Tools
ClassIn features an interactive electronic whiteboard, with pre-editing, multi-page scrolling, and unlimited use, supporting simultaneous operation by multiple authorized users.

It comes equipped with dozens of teaching tools — an interactive small blackboard, answer clicker, timer, teacher screen share, video wall, random student picker, and more — making it easy for teachers to run different types of classroom instruction.

### 5. Teaching Courseware Support
ClassIn supports a wide variety of interactive teaching courseware, including animated PPTs, video, audio, Word, and PDF, and also supports combining multiple courseware types in a single lesson, meeting a wider range of teaching needs and enriching lesson content.

### 6. Classroom Atmosphere and Management Features
ClassIn offers classroom management features like raise hand, buzzer, awarding rewards, and sending students on/off stage, helping create a lively, competitive classroom atmosphere that boosts student participation and enthusiasm.

Its real-time learning report system makes it easy for teachers and students to communicate outside of class hours and track teaching quality, helping build a well-rounded learning ecosystem.

### 7. Not Limited by Geography
Against the backdrop of globalization, ClassIn shows remarkable adaptability and convenience. No matter where in the world you are, as long as you have a network connection, you can easily use ClassIn to learn or teach.

For students, this means that whether you're in a bustling international city or a remote, quiet village, on the vast African savanna or in a cold Arctic settlement, as long as you can connect to the network, you can open ClassIn and interact with teachers and classmates from around the world — the door to knowledge opens globally. You can take part in a wide variety of excellent courses, broaden your horizons, and grow together with people from different cultural backgrounds.

For teachers, ClassIn's global nature means teaching is no longer bound by geography. You can enroll students from different countries and regions and share your expertise and experience. Across the bridge of the network, the flame of knowledge spreads, crossing borders and cultural differences to achieve truly borderless education.

# II. What Can ClassIn Do for Teachers?

In modern education, online courses have become an indispensable part of the landscape. Whether an experienced retired teacher or a young educator, many people choose to teach on a well-regarded online education platform. So how can students find the right teacher for themselves more quickly? Online education courses not only bring teachers and students closer together, but also help teachers accomplish a range of important goals.

First, online education platforms give teachers more opportunities and resources. These platforms have a large user base, letting teachers reach far more students. Whether retired or newly starting out, teachers can use these platforms to interact with and teach students from all over the country. This gives teachers a much bigger stage to fully apply their teaching abilities and experience.

Second, online education systems come with a wide range of features that effectively improve teaching outcomes. A mature education platform makes the teaching process smoother and easier. Teachers can present courseware and documents directly through the system, without spending a lot of time writing on a physical board. In addition, teachers can assign homework and exam papers on the platform, and checking that work becomes much more convenient. This lets teachers focus on the content of their teaching, improving overall teaching quality.

In addition, online education systems can support dual-teacher tutoring, further strengthening collaboration among teachers. By setting up a lead teacher and an assisting teacher, the lead teacher's workload can be lightened, giving them more time and energy for lesson prep and improving teaching quality. The dual-teacher model can also foster a positive educational atmosphere among teachers, encouraging mutual growth. For educational institutions, this also helps attract more outstanding teachers and raise the overall standard of education.

In summary, online education platforms bring teachers many benefits. Through these platforms, teachers can reach more students and achieve their teaching goals; the platform's many features improve teaching outcomes and make the teaching process smoother and easier; and the dual-teacher model strengthens collaboration among teachers and raises overall education standards. Online education is therefore not only an important part of modern education, but also a powerful tool for teachers to achieve their personal goals.

# III. What Can ClassIn Do for Students?

**Flexible learning:** online education breaks through the limits of time and space, letting students learn according to their own schedule, no longer bound by fixed class times. This greatly benefits students' daily learning life.

**Improved learning efficiency:** online education platforms offer rich learning resources and diverse learning methods. Students can choose materials and methods suited to their own level and needs. In addition, online teaching offers real-time interaction and personalized tutoring, which helps improve learning efficiency.

**Suited to busy schedules:** for people with busy jobs and irregular study time, traditional offline education often can't meet their needs. The flexibility of online education lets these people learn according to their own schedule, better balancing work and study.

**Access to shared, quality educational resources:** online education can bring together outstanding teaching resources from around the world, giving students access to broader, more diverse learning content. Through online learning platforms, students can also communicate and discuss with other students, sharing learning insights and experiences, enriching the learning process.

In summary, online education offers significant benefits to learners by providing flexible learning options, improving learning efficiency, accommodating busy schedules, and sharing quality educational resources. That said, students still need good self-management skills and study habits to fully take advantage of what online education offers and achieve the results they're hoping for.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

# I. Outside the Classroom

Take a screenshot: Ctrl+Alt+C

Open the software: Ctrl+Alt+X

Send a message: Enter

# II. Inside the Classroom

### (1) Shortcut Differences Between Mac and Windows

The "Ctrl" key on Windows corresponds to the "Command" key on Mac.

The "Alt" key on Windows corresponds to the "Option" key on Mac.

The "Page Up/Down" keys on Windows correspond to "Fn+Up/Down" on Mac.

The "Home/End" keys on Windows correspond to "Fn+Left/Right" on Mac.

### (2) General Classroom Shortcuts

| Function | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Select All | Ctrl + A |
| Copy | Ctrl + C |
| Paste | Ctrl + V |
| Delete | Ctrl + D / Delete |
