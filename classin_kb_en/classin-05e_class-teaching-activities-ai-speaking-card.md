## AI Speaking Card

## I. What Is an "AI Speaking Card"?

Among the four core skill areas in English education — listening, reading, speaking, and writing — objectively scoring or evaluating reading and speaking has always been a challenge, since they involve multiple factors like pronunciation, vocabulary, intonation, grammar, and fluency. Manual grading is not only a heavy workload, but also demanding on a grader's stamina and focus. In addition, since a grader's state can vary, grading results can fluctuate, adding to an institution's operating costs. AI speech evaluation can comprehensively improve students' speaking ability, while giving teachers an efficient, time-saving grading solution.

## II. Creating an "AI Speaking Card"

### Applies to
Head teachers and teachers.

### Usage Notes
Teachers can create an AI Speaking Card within a class using a computer or tablet.

On the new speaking card screen, teachers can click "Add Card" to add a question — each question accepts one word or one sentence (up to 150 characters). Pressing Enter automatically generates audio and a definition; if the input is a single word, phonetic notation is also generated. Up to 100 practice questions can be added. Teachers can also click "Bulk Add" to add questions, entering card content separated by line breaks for each question; on computer, teachers can also import questions from Excel. In addition, teachers can use AI to add questions intelligently — enter specific requirements in the AI interface, click Generate, and apply the generated content to the speaking card.

When a teacher enters a word or sentence for a card, the system automatically generates the corresponding audio, phonetic notation, and definition. If the teacher isn't satisfied with the generated audio, phonetic notation, or definition, they can hover over the text box to delete it. After deleting, the teacher can click to regenerate it automatically, or enter it manually; for audio specifically, teachers can also re-record, upload audio, or regenerate it automatically. Phonetic notation and definition are optional — leaving them blank won't affect normal publishing or grading.

Questions can be reordered by dragging, so teachers can arrange them as needed.

**Settings available when creating an "AI Speaking Card":**

| Setting | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Set the AI Speaking Card's start and due time. |
| Grading | Set the AI Speaking Card's grading scheme and points. |
| Publish to | Publish the AI Speaking Card to a specific class, to some group members or specific students within a class, or to multiple classes at once. |
| Teacher | Assign the activity's teacher to receive related notifications. |
| Unit Topic | Choose which course the AI Speaking Card belongs to. Defaults to the current course. |
| Allow students to see each other's content and grades | Sets whether students can view each other's full content and grades.<br><br>Once enabled, you can choose "all visible" or "only excellent content and grades visible"; and the visibility timing can be set to "visible after submission," "visible after grading," or "visible after the activity ends." |
| Allow late submission after the activity ends | Once enabled, if a student didn't complete the AI Speaking Card on time, they can submit it after the activity has ended. |
| Allow newly joined students to submit | Once enabled, students who join the class before the AI Speaking Card activity ends can also take part in the already-published activity. |
| Unlock conditions | Add prerequisite activities that must be completed. If unlock conditions are added, students must complete them before starting this AI Speaking Card activity. |

### Notes
Non-mainland-China (+86) accounts do not support automatic definition generation.

## III. Editing an "AI Speaking Card"

Teachers using a computer or tablet can edit an AI Speaking Card, but what can be edited depends on the card's status.

For drafts and cards that haven't started, "Course" cannot be changed; the card content and settings can both be edited.

For cards currently in progress, "Start Time" and "Course" in the settings cannot be changed. Whether the card content can be edited depends on whether any student has already recorded a practice reading for a question: if a student has recorded, the teacher cannot edit that card's content; if no student has recorded yet, the teacher can still edit it.

For cards that have ended, the card content can no longer be edited, and the following settings can no longer be changed: time, publish to, teacher, course, allow newly joined students to submit, and unlock conditions.

1. Go to the course.
2. Find the AI Speaking Card, click "···," then select Edit.

## VI. Duplicating an "AI Speaking Card"

AI Speaking Card activities can be duplicated to the same class or other classes, whether they're a draft, not started, in progress, or ended. Except for the start and due time, the duplicated card's content and settings default to match the original.

Duplicating an AI Speaking Card activity is only supported on computer and tablet.

1. Find the AI Speaking Card, click "···," then select Duplicate Activity.

## VII. Sharing an "AI Speaking Card"

Teachers can share an in-progress or ended AI Speaking Card to WeChat or other platforms, making it easier to prompt students to record and submit on time.

When a teacher shares an AI Speaking Card from a computer, they can share it to a ClassIn class or friend, or copy the link, or scan a WeChat QR code with their phone to share to WeChat friends or groups.

When a teacher shares an AI Speaking Card from a phone or tablet, they can share it to a ClassIn class or friend, or to WeChat friends/groups/Moments, Weibo, or QQ friends, and can also copy the link to share with others.

When viewed outside the ClassIn app, the shared content supports playing the teacher-published speaking card audio; to record and submit, students still need to launch ClassIn and join the class.

1. Tap AI Speaking Card.
2. Click the Share button in the top-right corner.
3. Select a sharing method.

## VIII. Reminding Students to Record the "AI Speaking Card"

After a teacher opens the AI Speaking Card page, they can see under the "Not Submitted" or "Sent Back" tabs which students still need to submit a recording, and can remind all students who need to submit.

The "Notify" reminder is a push notification through both the ClassIn WeChat official account and the ClassIn app, sent at 15-minute intervals.

"Copy Roster" copies a text-and-link list for manual reminding.

## IX. Students Submitting an "AI Speaking Card"

Once a teacher publishes an AI Speaking Card, students can find it in the ClassIn class or their to-dos, view it, and record and submit their reading.

On the AI Speaking Card reading screen, students can view each question, play the system's reference audio, and record themselves reading it aloud. After reading a given question, the system automatically gives a star rating and highlights inaccurately pronounced words in color (red for poor pronunciation or a skipped word, yellow for good, and green for excellent — see the image below); students can also play back their own recording, or choose to re-record or move on to the next question.

![](https://cofile.eeo.cn/res-store%2Fa961be4d21bccf95ac4457c976a94ed1cc3245f88a29868fa7ad39dd2b51d0c9_13405?q-sign-algorithm=sha1&q-ak=AKIDJQGsEOK2TfbFdEZifMnxrWx85mpdHj6H&q-sign-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-key-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-header-list=host&q-url-param-list=&q-signature=f5556b5b9c6afb98a686330e913bfb42c9354f8f)

If a student exits the reading page without submitting, the system saves their progress so they can continue recording the remaining questions next time.

Once a student finishes recording all questions, they can tap Submit; if grading is enabled, the score will be shown after submission.

Once a student submits their AI Speaking Card, they can still re-record and resubmit as long as the teacher hasn't manually graded it yet. If the teacher requests a re-recording when grading, they can mark the student's card as "Sent Back for Revision" — the student can then re-record their submitted card and resubmit it.

1. View the reading content.
2. Tap Start Reading.
3. On the reading page, start/end recording, view the star rating for the question, and play the system's or your own audio.
4. Once finished with a question, tap to switch to the next one.
5. Once all questions are recorded, tap Submit.

## XI. Grading an "AI Speaking Card"

Once a student submits an AI Speaking Card, teachers can find it in the class or their to-dos to grade.

If the AI Speaking Card has a grading scheme set, the system automatically generates a score based on the student's reading results (see the image below — the system automatically gives a star rating for every question and highlights inaccurately pronounced words in color), and teachers can re-review the student's card under "Graded." If the AI Speaking Card doesn't have a grading scheme set, teachers can grade the student's card under "Pending Grading."

![](https://cofile.eeo.cn/res-store%2F66731356977d1622a2c41805a381bde7ebced23b9b6e3dd9d5d3ca08b9a89672_21288?q-sign-algorithm=sha1&q-ak=AKIDJQGsEOK2TfbFdEZifMnxrWx85mpdHj6H&q-sign-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-key-time=1773936000;1774540800&q-header-list=host&q-url-param-list=&q-signature=a73e36898ebc259d418351f1739f64ef0587764c)

While grading, teachers can play a student's recording for each question and comment based on it; comments support both text and voice recordings. If a student's reading is exceptional, teachers can mark it as "Excellent," and can also assign a grade if grading is enabled.

Once teachers finish grading, they can click Submit. If a student needs to re-record and resubmit, teachers can click Send Back for Revision.

Teachers can grade a student's submitted card multiple times; the student's history retains records of their submissions, the system's automatic grading, and the teacher's grading. If teachers don't want a student to see certain grading records, they can delete them from the student's history.

1. Go to Pending Grading or Graded.
2. Click Grade or Re-Grade.
3. Play the student's recording, then leave a comment.
4. Click Send Back for Revision, or Submit Grading.

## XII. Viewing/Downloading "AI Speaking Card" Data

On the AI Speaking Card's details page, teachers can view overall reading analysis and student data for the current card; on computer, they can also download detailed student data.

**Reading analysis:** teachers can click on the bar chart to jump directly to a specific question card, making it easier to identify problems common across the class. On phone, reading analysis appears under "Reading Content & Data" on the activity details page.

**Student data:** includes the current AI Speaking Card's overall submission rate, highest score, average score, and number of "Excellent" ratings.

Teachers using a computer can download all data — that is, detailed student data — locally after the student data section; the data includes each student's submission, grading, and score information.

Phone and tablet can only view reading analysis and student data, without downloading detailed student data.

1. View Student Data and Reading Data.
2. Download detailed student data.
